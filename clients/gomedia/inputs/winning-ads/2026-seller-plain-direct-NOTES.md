## Exact copy (5 lines)
1. Property agents 👋  — audience call-out, black bold
2. **10 verified seller leads a month** — the offer, big bold RED
3. `from $500/month` — price in a solid YELLOW box
4. Tampines, Bishan, Toa Payoh, Queenstown + 35 more — specificity, regular weight
5. Message us to start 👇 — CTA pointing to the button below

## Why it works
- Looks like a friend's Facebook text post, not an ad → no "ad blindness", stops the scroll by being unusually plain.
- Reads in ~2 seconds: who (agents) → what (10 verified seller leads) → how much ($500) → where (real towns) → do what (message).
- Only 3 visual levels: black, red, yellow box. Nothing competes.
- Real SG town names make it concrete and local.
- Fixed price + fixed number = no risk/maths for the agent.
