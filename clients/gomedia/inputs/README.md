# What to collect — GoMedia

GoMedia is B2B: sells LeadX lead-gen systems (buyer leads, seller leads, telemarketer/ISA system, CRM) to Singapore property agents.

Drop files into the matching subfolder:

- **reviews/** — agent testimonials and results (deals closed, lead volume, before/after).
- **comments/** — agent questions and objections pulled from WhatsApp and sales chats (e.g. "are the leads real / shared with other agents?").
- **winning-ads/** — the best-performing "Direct version" ads — both the ad text and the ad image.
- **competitors/** — (not specified by name above, but useful) ads or offers from competing lead-gen providers or agencies, if you have them.
- **product-images/** — logo and product screenshots (LeadX dashboard, CRM, telemarketer system, WhatsApp follow-up flow).
- **ad-references/** — ad formats you want cloned or reused for new creative.
- **fact-sheets/** — not typically needed for GoMedia (that's a GoMedia Homes thing), leave empty unless you have a specific offer fact sheet.
- **General/root of inputs/** or wherever it fits — LeadX Terms & Conditions and the lead replacement policy (needed to back the "guaranteed leads" and "replacement for bad leads" claims in brand.md).

## Before you save chat screenshots or exports
**Remove phone numbers and personal names before saving chats** into `comments/` or `reviews/`.
