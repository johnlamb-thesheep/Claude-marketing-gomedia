## Plain / lo-fi format: what the sources support
Every search engine tried (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Marginalia) returned bot checks or unrelated results. Practitioner articles on "ugly ads" (e.g. runneth.com/motionapp.com, foreplay.co, semrush.com) returned an index page or a 404. **So this section has no fetched practitioner source.** What it does have:

- **Ad-like styling gets ignored.** In NN/g's eye-tracking work, users skip content based on "visual treatment". Promos that looked "different from the text and the photography on the site" were easy to dismiss. (Pernice, NN/g, 2018: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/banner-blindness-old-and-new-findings/)
- **Banner blindness:** users "consciously or subconsciously ignore banner-like information". Clutter increases avoidance. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banner_blindness)
- **Native-format effect:** ads in "the style and format of the content near the advertisement's placement" are linked to "increased attention" and less ad avoidance (Jung & Heo 2019, cited at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_advertising). In a feed full of text posts, a plain text post is the native format.
- **Isolation effect:** one item that stands apart is "more likely to be remembered" (von Restorff, 1933: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Restorff_effect). This supports a single red line and a single yellow box, not several accents.
- **Fluency:** information "processed more fluently" is judged higher on the criterion in question (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluency_heuristic). This supports 5 short lines readable in about 2 seconds.
- **Our own account data (strongest evidence):** "Direct" framing beat the alternatives in 3 product lines: buyer S$17.77 vs S$34.64/lead, ISA S$24.28 vs S$36.41, seller S$23.03 vs S$24.36 (all low/low-medium confidence, small n). Source: `clients/gomedia/learnings.md`, 2026-11-03.

## Observations vs interpretations
**Observations (what the data shows):**
- 5 of 14 SG competitor/our headlines are number-led. ZenProp uses "cost in / result out" or "volume + timeframe" ([1829828228146494](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1829828228146494), [1790210512252191](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1790210512252191)).
- Only GoMedia puts a fixed monthly price in the headline ([1771810017434435](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1771810017434435)).
- No competitor headline names a location or town (Ad Library pull file).
- ZenProp's numbers are past results (14 viewings, 65 enquiries). Ours is a forward offer (10 leads for $500).
- gomedia.sg shows no prices, lead counts, or locations. It shows "200+ agents" and "7,000+ appointments set" (https://gomedia.sg).
- The "Direct" framing won in all 3 product lines tested, with small samples (learnings.md).
- The reference ad (`inputs/winning-ads/2026-seller-plain-direct-REFERENCE.png`) uses 5 centered lines on white: black call-out, red offer, yellow price box, regular-weight towns, bold CTA.
- brand.md §5a asks for a small red all-caps "PROPERTY AGENT" eyebrow. The winning reference uses "Property agents 👋" as a plain black line instead.

**Interpretations (our read on why, and what to do about it):**
- The fixed price plus fixed quantity is our sharpest differentiator in this auction. ZenProp sells outcomes, we sell a known cost. Keep "from $500/month" and "10 verified seller leads a month" in every variant, as the brief requires.
- Town names are uncontested territory. No competitor uses them, so they are worth keeping and could be tested as the hero line (V6).
- ZenProp's "In / Out" phrasing shows the audience has already seen and responds to plain maths. A per-lead line ("about $50 a lead", derived from $500 ÷ 10) competes on the same terms without copying them. Note that it is a "from" price, so "$50" is a floor and needs "from".
- The "200+ agents / 7,000+ appointments" proof is the only quantified social proof available. It is the biggest unused asset, but it has to be confirmed as current before use.
- For the plain look to work, any UI mimicry (notes app, iMessage, FB post) has to stay as plain as the reference. Adding app chrome can tip it back into "designed ad". QA should also check Meta's policy on non-functional UI elements (e.g. fake buttons or notification badges) before launch.
- The "PROPERTY AGENT" eyebrow rule (brand.md §5a) conflicts with the reference's "Property agents 👋" line. The copywriter should keep the reference's version for the control family. Whether the plain variants also get the eyebrow is for performance or the user to decide, not research.

## Plain-format variation playbook
Fixed anchors in every variant: white background; plain Arial/Helvetica; black text plus at most one red line and one yellow box; "from $500/month" exactly; "10 verified seller leads a month"; real towns only (only **Tampines, Bishan, Toa Payoh, Queenstown + 35 more** is sourced, so any other town needs the client to confirm coverage); no "LeadX"; no "free lead replacement" / "WhatsApp automation included"; no agency names; no "guaranteed"; 4:5 1080×1350.

Facts used and their sources: 10 verified seller leads / from $500/month / towns come from the reference ad and brand.md §3. $2,000/month agency comes from brand.md §1 and §5a. "Say goodbye to leadless days" comes from gomedia.sg/lead-generation. "Fixed price" comes from gomedia.sg/lead-generation. 200+ agents and 7,000+ appointments come from gomedia.sg and need confirmation. "Project buyer" comes from our live ad 2348003596047550.
