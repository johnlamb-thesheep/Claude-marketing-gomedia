# Creative Brief — 2026-11 Direct / Plain-text statics (LeadX seller + buyer leads)

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Client:** gomedia
**Author:** lead (from the user's direct request in chat — strategist stage skipped at the user's instruction)

## Goal
Make new variations of the proven "Direct" plain-text static (see `inputs/winning-ads/2026-seller-plain-direct-NOTES.md`) — both copy-wise and design-wise — to refresh it without losing what made it work: a super-basic format that grabs attention by looking like a plain Facebook text post.

## KPI
Cost per lead at or below the current Direct seller ad (~S$23/lead, learnings.md 2026-11-03). Secondary: CTR (link) vs. the reference.

## ICP segment
Singapore property agents — mainly resale/seller agents (seller leads); buyer-leads (new launch agents) versions allowed as a secondary set.

## Awareness stage
Solution-aware (they know lead gen exists, they've seen ZenProp/agency ads).

## Objection to overcome
"Is it worth it / are the leads real?" — answered by a fixed number, fixed price, "verified" and real town names.

## Angles (max 3) — all inside the Direct plain-text format
### Angle 1 — Direct offer (control family)
- **Hypothesis:** Same 5-line structure, swapping one element per variant (hook line, hero number, location list, CTA) isolates what drives the win.
- **Proof:** "Direct" won in 3 product lines (learnings.md).
### Angle 2 — Per-lead maths / cost contrast
- **Hypothesis:** "$50 a seller lead" or a hard dollar contrast vs. a typical agency/portal spend reads in 1 second and beats vague value claims.
- **Proof:** brand.md 5a (concrete numbers beat vague ones); competitor ZenProp leads with "S$664 In. 14 Viewings Out."
### Angle 3 — Scarcity / plain question hook
- **Hypothesis:** A plain, factual scarcity or question line ("No new listings this month?") in the same plain format adds a reason to act now.
- **Proof:** gomedia-static-ad-rules — scarcity framing was a client "hit".

## Conversion path
Ad → Meta instant form / WhatsApp message (existing).

## Must / must-not
- Must: white background, plain Arial/Helvetica, black + one red line + yellow price box max; "from $500/month" exact wording; real SG town names; 4:5 1080×1350 (plus 9:16 story versions where useful); ~5 short lines; emoji allowed (👋 👇 ✅).
- Must not: "LeadX" in the creative (client asked for generic we/us); "free lead replacement" / "WhatsApp automation included" boilerplate; cheesy lines ("Which area do you farm?"); guaranteed sales/commissions; naming agencies (ERA, PropNex…); design elements that make it look like a "designed ad".
