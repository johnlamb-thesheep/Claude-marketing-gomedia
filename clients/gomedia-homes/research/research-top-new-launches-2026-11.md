## How this was built (read first)

- **Primary % sold source: newlaunches.sg project pages**, each showing "X of Y units sold", stamped "Updated 06-Nov-2026" or "07-Nov-2026" [NL]. That is a developer-availability feed run by an agent portal, **not URA data**. I tested it against news and Stacked Homes figures. It matches for most projects, but it is **stale or wrong for some** (see caveats). The worst case is Thomson Reserve, which it still shows as 0% sold although news reports 90.1% sold on launch weekend.
- **Cross-check sources:** Stacked Homes, EdgeProp, Business Times, Straits Times and 99.co launch-sales reports, plus URA/Huttons September 2026 best-sellers (published 15 Oct 2026). URA's October 2026 developer sales are **not out yet** (normally released mid-November), so September is the latest monthly project-level data.
- **Consumer-demand signals:** EdgeProp's weekly "most-searched new launch condos" (data to 20 Oct and 28 Oct 2026) and "most-transacted new launches, last 14 days" [EP-20, EP-28]; URA/Huttons Sept 2026 top sellers [SH-SEP]; launch-weekend take-up; newlaunches.sg "Popular Projects This Week" [NL-HOME].
- **Agent-side signals:** No agent-facing co-broke or commission data was publicly accessible. **List B is a proxy ranking**: units left to sell (inventory) × recent sales momentum × entry quantum (≤~$1.6M is easier to close and can lead with price under our rules) × visible developer marketing push. Treat it as a data-led shortlist, not a strategy call.
- **Hard filter:** anything at ≥90% sold or fully sold is excluded. Projects whose % sold I couldn't confirm are listed separately as UNVERIFIED.
- newlaunchsg.com is a parked placeholder with no data. newlaunchsg.sg failed with an SSL error (self-signed certificate). Only newlaunches.sg worked.

## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations (what the data shows):**
- Thomson Reserve dominated buyer searches in both EdgeProp weeks (#1) and sold 90.1% on its 30–31 Oct 2026 launch weekend. The units left are 2BR and 2BR Premium ($1.635M–$2.217M) plus 2 × 5BR [SH-TR].
- Half of EdgeProp's top-10 searched names to 28 Oct are already fully sold or above 90% (Pinetree Hill, Hillock Green, Parktown, The Continuum, Lentor Hills; plus The Myst, unchecked) [EP-28, NL-BROWSE].
- The Sept 2026 URA best-sellers were mostly older launches: Terra Hill 32, The Sen 25, Amberwood 24, Lentor Gardens 22, Union Square 21. New-home sales in Sept were 296 units [SH-SEP].
- Coastal Cabana sold 19 of the 23 EC units sold island-wide in Sept. 142 EC units were unsold at end-Sept [SH-SEP].
- Among verified projects below 90%, those with an entry price of ~$1.5M or below are Lucerne Grand, Vela Bay, The Sen, Dunearn House, Union Square, Promenade Peak, Narra Residences and Coastal Cabana EC.

**Interpretations (my read; strategist to decide):**
- Buyer interest is chasing projects that are already sold out. Ads on in-stock projects next to the "hot" names (Lucerne Grand, Vela Bay, Lentor Gardens) may catch spill-over demand. This is unproven.
- Thomson Reserve buyers who missed out (larger units are 100% sold) may be an audience for comparable in-stock family units. That is a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
- The Serra and Amberwood draw high transaction counts and attention but have high entry prices, so price-led Direct ads are not allowed for them under client rules.

## Data caveats

1. **The NL figures are an agent-portal feed, not URA.** They match the news for Lucerne Grand, Vela Bay, Lentor Gardens, Dunearn House, Coastal Cabana, Pinery, River Modern, Narra and Newport (allowing for sales since the news date). They are **wrong or stale** for Thomson Reserve (0% vs 90.1%), Chuan Park (0/916 while it is selling), Bloomsbury (self-contradictory) and probably TMW Maxwell. Several NL pages also contradict themselves on unit counts (J'den, The Orie unit tables; Wynwood Grand 430 vs 420; Solano Grand 300 vs 295).
2. **Union Square Residences** has a conflict: NL 79% vs Stacked "over 175 of 366" (~48%+) at 22 Aug. Both are below 90%, so it passes the filter, but quote neither figure in ads.
3. **Coastal Cabana (85%)** and **Kassia (86%), ELTA (87%)** are close to the 90% cap. Recheck before committing budget.
4. **Price/psf columns:** "from" prices are the cheapest listed unit on NL. "From psf" is often a different, larger unit. Prices change; confirm against the developer price list before using them in ads, with the disclaimer.
5. **MRT distances** are NL's approximate figures. They are not verified, and our rules restrict walk-time claims in ads.
6. **List B is a proxy.** No co-broke, commission or agent-sentiment data was accessible.
7. **Monthly sales:** the latest URA project-level data is September 2026. October 2026 data is expected mid-November.
8. Nothing here has gone through the Meta Ad Library. Competitor ad activity per project is not covered in this file.
