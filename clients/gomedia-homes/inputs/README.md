# What to collect — GoMedia Homes (ads run as "GoAhead")

GoMedia Homes is B2C: runs ads for Singapore new launch condo and EC buyers.

Drop files into the matching subfolder:

- **reviews/** — buyer reviews (experience with GoAhead / the consultation, not developer reviews).
- **comments/** — buyer WhatsApp questions and objections (affordability, timing, ABSD, progressive payment doubts, etc.).
- **winning-ads/** — the Progressive Payment ad and the Sembawang ad — both the ad text and the ad image.
- **competitors/** — competing agencies' or developers' ads for the same launches, if available.
- **product-images/** — logo and project renders **we have rights to use** — do not add renders pulled from the web without checking rights first.
- **ad-references/** — ad formats you want cloned or reused for new creative.
- **fact-sheets/** — **one fact sheet per project**, filename like `<project-name>.md` or `.pdf`, each covering: price (from), tenure, total units, MRT distance, developer.

## Before you save chat screenshots or exports
**Remove phone numbers and personal names before saving chats** into `comments/` or `reviews/`.
