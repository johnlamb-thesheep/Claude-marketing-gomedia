# Competitor check — bedroom-count / price-anchor new-launch ad format

Date: 2026-11-04 | Source: Meta Ad Library (SG, HOUSING_ADS + general, ad_active_status=ACTIVE) | Run by: researcher (ad-hoc, not tied to a specific campaign cycle)

**Question asked:** is anyone else running an ad like GoMedia Homes' "New Launch Custom List of Home" set (bedroom-count headline + price anchor + benefit icons + GET QUOTE / DOWNLOAD LIST)?

## Observations vs interpretations

### Observations (what the data shows)
- Searching "new launch condo Singapore" returned 32 active ads; searching "bedder price list" returned 24 more, with heavy overlap on format.
- GoMedia Homes' own page ("Gomedia Homes and Properties") appears in results with 2 active ads: "Download Full Price List and E-brochure" and "Get full price list & E-brochure."
- At least 8 other pages are running the **same structural format** (bedroom count + price anchor, sometimes + MRT proximity) right now:
  - **Before You Buy** — running 3+ simultaneous variants: "Brand new 5 bedder near the MRT, from $3.63M," "New 4 bedder near the MRT, under $2.8M," "Last 3 bedders by the MRT from $2,680 psf." Multiple ad IDs per headline (duplicate creative running in parallel) — reads as active A/B testing of this exact formula.
  - **Lex Lim Property** — "Brand-New 1-Bedders From $1.1M | Homes From $1.1M | Find Your Home Within Budget." Near-identical price point and phrasing to GoMedia's own `ref-05-1bedroom-price-anchor.jpg` ("1-BEDROOM HOMES FROM $1.1M").
  - **KJ Heng Real Estate** — "LAST CHANCE to secure a NEW LAUNCH 4-Bedder below $2.3M!" / "...3-Bedder below $1.6M!" — same format, stacked with urgency language.
  - **SG Undervalued Developments** — one ad listing all bedroom tiers at once: "2 Bedroom Under $1.3M | 3 Bedroom Under $1.5M | 4 Bedroom Under $2.2M | 5 Bedroom Under $2.5M." Two versions of this exact ad found at different price points across different periods (2026 vs an earlier run at slightly different numbers), suggesting it's a recurring, periodically-refreshed evergreen ad.
  - **Judy's Property Page** — "North 2-Bedder Nearing TOP, from $1.881M."
  - **Homes with Charissa** — "Get an 'Exclusive List' of Beautiful 4-Bedroom Condos!" — list lead-magnet variant (matches GoMedia's `ref-06` DOWNLOAD LIST format).
  - **Singjia Property** — ad title repeats "Click To Get Your List Today!" nine times in the same creative link title (keyword/CTA stuffing).
  - **Hot Properties Of SG** — "Click To Get Discounts | E-brochure | Price Lists | Floor Plans" — near-identical phrasing to GoMedia's own ad titles.
  - **Properties Hunting** — "Undervalued 4 Bedder Condos! | Looking for bigger units and bedrooms? This might be for you!"
- **SGProperty** runs a long-lived evergreen ad ("Discover Top Below Market Condo Deals For This Year!") with ad creation timestamps spanning from roughly a year ago through this week — same title relaunched repeatedly over a long period, which usually signals a format the advertiser considers a reliable performer, not a one-off test.
- A smaller cluster uses a **comparison framing** instead of bedroom+price: Jackson B & Associates ("Same layout, $173,000 apart") and Chris Yan Property ("Side by side") — structurally different from GoMedia's set, fewer competitors doing this.
- Most results are individual property agents/consultants (not developers) — matches GoMedia Homes' own positioning as a buyer-consultation intermediary, not a developer.

### Interpretations (read with appropriate caution — small sample, headline text only, no view into actual spend/results)
- The bedroom-count + price-anchor format is **saturated**, not a differentiator on its own — at least 8 competitors are running near-identical structural ads concurrently. GoMedia's visual execution (benefit-icon row, trust footer, real photo collages) may be the actual point of difference, not the headline formula itself.
- "Before You Buy" is the closest direct structural competitor and appears to be the most systematic tester of this exact format (3 simultaneous bedroom-tier variants).
- The repeated, long-running SGProperty ad and the KJ Heng urgency variants suggest both "evergreen discovery" and "urgency/scarcity" framings have staying power in this specific format — worth considering as test angles if GoMedia Homes wants to diversify beyond the plain bedroom+price headline.
- Comparison-style hooks ("same layout, $X apart") look like relative white space — few competitors are doing this, and it's a genuinely different mechanic from the bedroom+price list that dominates the rest of the field.
- Could not visually inspect the actual ad creative (Facebook's ad library snapshot pages returned HTTP 403 to automated browsing) — this report is based on ad titles/page names/timing only, not full creative text or imagery. A manual look at the snapshot URLs below would confirm whether visual execution also converges on GoMedia's format (benefit icons, photo collage, trust footer) or differs.
