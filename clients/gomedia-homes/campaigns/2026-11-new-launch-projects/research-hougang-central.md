## Price rule outcome

**Price does NOT go in any Hougang ad for now. That includes the Direct ad.**

- The rule needs a **confirmed entry price of $1.4M or less**. **No official, developer or indicative price list exists** on any source I reached.
- The only figures are analyst estimates: an **average above $2,500 psf** (PropNex via [SH1]), with **2BR above $1.5M** and 3BR well above $2M [SH2]. On those estimates a 2BR would break the cap.
- The unit mix is unknown. If the project has 1BR / 1BR+Study units of ~500 sqft, $2,500 psf would put them around $1.25M, under the cap. That is **my arithmetic on an unconfirmed mix and an analyst psf. It is not a price.** It must not appear anywhere.
- The **Price slot** becomes **"Price Coming Soon / Be first to know"** (per SKILL.md). No number, and no "estimate" wording in the ad either, because the client rule is stricter than the skill: no price anywhere unless it's confirmed at $1.4M or less.
- **Re-check when the developer releases prices.** If a confirmed entry is ≤$1.4M, the Direct ad switches to leading with price.

## CTA recommendation

**"REGISTER YOUR INTEREST"** on all ads.
- The project is pre-launch with no released price list. "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE" would promise something that doesn't exist, which is a broken first touch (SKILL.md, CTA for project ads).
- About 11 competitor pages are already making that empty "price list" promise, so an honest "be first to know when prices are out" is also a small differentiator.
- **Our own two live ads ("Download Full Price List and E-brochure", 11-02 and 11-05) have this problem.** That's a downstream fix; see conflicts.
- Switch to "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE" only once the developer releases a price list.
- Meta button suggestion: "Sign up" / "Get updates" style, matched to the banner. This is copy/LP's call.

- Three newlaunches.sg URLs for this name serve a different project. One agent domain has wrong and inconsistent data. [as above]
- Creative bodies, visuals and CTAs could not be seen (Facebook 403).

**Interpretations (our read, and what to do about it):**
- **Saturated:**
  - "integrated / above MRT" as a bare claim
  - price list / e-brochure magnets
  - VIP/VVIP/register
  - landmark / central core
  - "mega" / "first in X years" (also banned for us)
  - D19 framing (banned for us)
- **The MRT is still the hottest USP, but the white space is in *how* it's said.** Competitors say "integrated with MRT". Nobody says what that buys you: 2 stops to Serangoon MRT for the Circle Line, and a second line (CRL, from 2030) that makes this the only NEL–CRL interchange. Specific beats generic, as the Arcady "6-MIN" finding showed.
- **Real white space:**
  1. Commute maths and the dual-line future.
  2. The planned mall at your doorstep.
  3. The stay-in-Hougang upgrader.
  4. Family and schools.
  5. Emotional, need-led hooks. Every competitor headline in the pull is project-led or offer-led.
- **The "mega/8 years" claim is shaky.** Florence launched in 2019, Chuan Park is listed as D19, and a Hougang landed launch came in Oct 2026. If GoAhead wants a scarcity proof, the defensible version is "first major Hougang condo launch since 2019" [SH1]. Even that should sit in primary text with a source date, not in a hook. The SKILL already lists "first major Hougang launch since 2018/2019" as acceptable sourced proof.
- **Name risk:** "Hougang Central Residences" isn't confirmed. If the developer announces a different name, the Direct ad and the form intro must change, and name-search traffic will move. Keep the Direct ad's name in a swappable text layer.
- **Lead-quality risk:** with no price, every ad pulls mixed budgets. The form's budget question is the filter (same as Amber House and Arcady). Based on the analyst estimate only, a cut-off band near S$1.5M looks sensible. Confirm it when prices are released.
- **Many pages carrying the project or developer names** could be agents impersonating the developer, or appointed agencies. That's unverified either way. GoAhead should not imply developer status (same issue as Amber House C1-1).
- **Pre-launch images:** no building exists, so every exterior or facility visual is an artist's impression. Use the official developer render if the client has the rights, labelled "IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY". A generated generic image must say "IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY", per the Arcady T10 precedent, so it doesn't pass as this project.
