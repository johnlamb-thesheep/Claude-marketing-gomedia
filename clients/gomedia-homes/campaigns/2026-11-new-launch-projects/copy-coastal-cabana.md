**Fixed on every ad:**
- Eyebrow `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER`.
- CTA `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE`. The price list is released and current ([RES] §7). The Meta button should be **"Download"**, matched to the banner, not GET_QUOTE (perf S5).
- The CTA promises **the current balance-unit list (3-bedroom types, plus the single 5-bedroom on request)**. It never promises 4-bedroom units.
- `EC eligibility conditions apply.` is a sentence in the **primary text of every ad**, and it also leads the on-image fine print.
- Stage wording: "show flat open for viewing" only. Never "preview", "VVIP", "launching soon", "register before launch" or "be the first" ([RES] §1, §8.10).
- Base on-image fine print: `EC eligibility conditions apply. 99-year leasehold. Distances approx., straight-line.` Ads that mention the Cross Island Line add `Cross Island Line targeted for 2030; timeline subject to change.` Ads that mention the Deferred Payment Scheme add `DPS prices differ from normal-scheme prices; terms per developer.` C2 adds the MOE line.
- Image label (perf T10 precedent):
  - `*IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY*` **only** on an official developer render of this project, once rights are confirmed (perf S7).
  - Any generic, stock or generated image gets `*IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development*`.
  - The project is under construction. No AI building is ever captioned or implied as this project (perf C1-1). No Coney Island imagery.
- Leave a strip above the CTA for the CEA particulars (perf S3).

**Hard exclusions (checked on every line, fine print included):**
- No price, "$", psf, monthly figure, discount or grant amount. The developer's $10–12k discount is price-adjacent and is not used.
- No unit counts, block counts or units-per-floor. No % sold, "remaining units", "last units", "few left" or any scarcity.
- No district code. No street names (Jalan Loyang Besar, Pasir Ris Drive 6, Eunos Avenue 3). No "Loyang" (area name in copy is "Pasir Ris" only).
- "EC" or "executive condominium" only. Never "freehold", "condo" alone, or "private condo".
- No walk minutes and no metre figures. Straight-line distances are always "near/nearby". The only positional word stronger than "near" is "opposite Downtown East" ([SHP] "directly opposite"; see Flag 9).
- Full MRT names always: Pasir Ris MRT, Pasir Ris East MRT, Hougang MRT, Ang Mo Kio MRT. "Cross Island Line" and "East-West Line" in full. Pasir Ris East MRT is always paired with "Cross Island Line" and "2030 (target)".
- **Only C1 says "Coastal Cabana"** and names the developers (primary text only). C2–C7 leave out the name, the developers, the developer entity and the address.
- No Coney Island.
- No gain, resale-profit, privatisation, "Sell 1 Buy 2", "instant equity", "cheaper than Canberra" or "CRL will boost value" framing. Privatisation is not mentioned at all ([RES] §8.6, §8.9).
- MOP and DPS appear only as plain facts. Never "the last 5-year-MOP EC" and never "one of the last few" (inaccurate / urgency, [RES] §8.7).
- Sea views only as "sea views from selected units". The developer's "only sea-view EC in 12 years" is not used.
- No "$18,000" income ceiling anywhere (it doesn't apply to this project, [SHI]).

**Why C7 stays an MRT ad but becomes a neighbourhood map:**
- **The MRT is this project's weak fact, not a USP** ([RES] USP rank 6, strength 2). Pasir Ris MRT is ~0.9km straight-line, and Stacked says it's "not exactly walkable". A minutes headline is impossible, and a bare "near Pasir Ris MRT" ad would be the weakest in the set.
- **A map lets the MRT ride with the strong facts.** The two stations sit on the same graphic as Downtown East, the beach, Pasir Ris Park and White Sands, so the location reads as a whole.
- **The Cross Island Line is genuinely unused.** 0 competitor headlines mention it or Pasir Ris East MRT ([RES] §3), and Pasir Ris MRT itself becomes a CRL interchange ([WKC]).
- The map format also isn't used anywhere else in this set.
- Alternative I considered and didn't pick: an "airport-side" commute ad (Changi Airport ~8–22 min drive at peak). The drive range is wide and only one source gives it, so it stays as a supporting line in C7's primary text.
