## PROJECT FACTS (shared reference)
1. **One Marina Gardens** — D01, Marina South, 99-yr leasehold, from $2,781 psf, from $1.876M, 2-4BR. CTA confirmed by client: "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE."
2. **Amber House** — D15 (East Coast/Marine Parade), 30 Amber Gardens, Freehold, Far East Organization, 105 units/16-storey tower, 2-4BR 538-1,744 sqft, from approx. $2,950 psf (sources disagree $2,930-$3,030), entry price not confirmed, TOP 2029, launched ~June 2025.
3. **Keppel Bay Plot 6 (marketed as "Keppel Island")** — D04, Keppel Bay Drive, near HarbourFront MRT, 99-yr leasehold, Keppel Land, ~86 units, TOP ~2027. Price/psf TBA. Name not fully confirmed — "Keppel Bay Plot 6" primary, "(Keppel Island)" secondary.
4. **One Leonie Residences** — D9 (Orchard/River Valley), Leonie Hill, Freehold, Far East Organization, unit count disputed (37 vs 70), TOP ~2028. Price/psf not confirmed. Neighbouring Leonie Hill Residences resale ($2,400-$2,550 psf) = area context only, never this project's price.
5. **Hougang Central Residences** — D19, 99-yr leasehold, TOP ~2030, site awarded Jan 2026, tender ~S$1.5B. First major Hougang launch since The Florence Residences/Riverfront Residences (2018/2019). Price not official — analyst estimate ~$2,500-$2,600+ psf.
6. **The Arcady at Boon Keng** — D12, Serangoon Road, Freehold, KSH Ultra Unity, 172 units, ~500m/5-min walk to Boon Keng MRT (NE9). From $2,390 psf (3BR), $2,500 psf (2BR), $2,510 psf (1BR+study), $2,560 psf (4BR+study). Entry ~$1.33M. TOP ~late 2028.
7. **Bagnall Haus** — D16, Bedok/Upper East Coast, Freehold, Roxy-Pacific-led consortium, 113 units. Sungei Bedok MRT "2 min walk." Launch avg $2,450 psf (Jan 2025). Entry $1.235M (1BR+flexi). Over 60% take-up on launch weekend, sold units averaging $2,490 psf. TOP ~2028.
8. **The Collective at One Sophia** — D9, 1 Sophia Road, 99-yr leasehold, SingHaiyi Group & Chuan Capital, 367 units/2 towers. Studio-3BR 431-1,249 sqft. From approx. $2,740 psf. Entry from approx. $1.212M (studio). TOP ~Dec 2029.

- 5 angles: 1 Direct · 2 Blind · 3 Floor layout · 4 MRT distance · 5 Price/PSF. 6th optional curiosity/story angle (higher risk) for One Marina Gardens only.

## Creative direction notes (from copy doc)
- Portrait 4:5 (1080x1350). Eyebrow "SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER" red top-left. White/black/red palette. Red CTA banner.
- No real product photography on file for any project. Launched / near-TOP projects (OMG, Amber House, Arcady, Bagnall Haus, One Sophia) → generic representative image labelled "IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY". Genuinely pre-launch (Keppel Bay Plot 6, One Leonie, Hougang Central) → photoreal architectural render labelled "IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY".
- OMG: Marina South/Marina Bay skyline for Direct/Price; waterfront district for Blind; city-centre skyline for MRT; construction/cranes for curiosity angle. MRT angle shows no station name/walk time.
- Floor-layout angles: floor-plan line drawing as hero + building image (both).
- KB-5: no digit, $ sign, or PSF text anywhere in the image.
- OL: no price, PSF, or unit count anywhere.
- HC-5: visible "ANALYST ESTIMATE" badge next to the $2,500+ PSF figure.
- AR-4: condo AND a generic unbranded MRT station entrance in the same frame, confident.
- AR-5: exact by-unit-type psf figures.
- BH-1: small red "60%+ SOLD" badge. BH-3: real floor plan + real photo composited (real assets needed in inputs/product-images/). BH-4: "2 min walk" stated confidently.
- CS-3: STUDIO floor plan (431-452 sqft) + exterior render. CS-4: D9/Orchard prestige, no invented station/distance.
- No images generated; no paid generation run or costed.
