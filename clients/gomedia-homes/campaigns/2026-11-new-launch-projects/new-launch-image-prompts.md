# GoMedia Homes — New Launch Project Ads — Image Prompts

8 projects × 5 angles (+ optional 6th for One Marina Gardens) = 41 prompts. Compiled 2026-11-04.

**How to use:** copy the text in the grey box under each angle and paste it into your image or video tool. Every prompt is complete on its own. Headline, subhead and CTA match the approved copy word for word.

**Shared look (already inside every prompt):** portrait 4:5 (1080x1350), white background, black text, red accent. Eyebrow "SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER" in red, top-left. Red CTA banner at the bottom. Image label: "IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY", or "IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY" for the 3 pre-launch projects.

**Tip:** image AIs often garble long text. If the words come out wrong, generate the picture only and add the text in Canva.
