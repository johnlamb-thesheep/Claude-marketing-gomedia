**Hard exclusions (checked on every line, fine print included):**
- No price, "$", psf, "estimate" or "analyst" wording.
- No unit counts. No "mega", "830", "first in 8 years" or "launch of the year".
- No % sold. No scarcity.
- No district code. No street names (Hougang Avenue 10). The phrase "Hougang Central" appears only in C1.
- No "freehold". The project is 99-year leasehold, written as "99-year leasehold" or not mentioned.
- No walk minutes and no drive times. MRT copy says "above Hougang MRT" only. Schools, parks and malls are "near/nearby", never minutes or metres.
- Full MRT names always: Hougang MRT, Serangoon MRT, Dhoby Ghaut MRT, Ang Mo Kio MRT, Pasir Ris MRT, Bright Hill MRT. That covers headlines, subheads, badges and diagram labels. "Cross Island Line" is written in full and paired with "2030 (target)".
- Only C1 says "Hougang Central Residences" and names UOL / CapitaLand (primary text only). C2–C7 leave out the project name, the developers, CICT and any mall name or brand.
- Saturated hooks are not used as bare claims: "integrated", "landmark", "central core", "icon", "mega", "price list", "VIP/VVIP". "Above Hougang MRT" only ever appears with stop counts or amenities.
- No "boost value" or implied-gains framing for the Cross Island Line (brand.md §5).
