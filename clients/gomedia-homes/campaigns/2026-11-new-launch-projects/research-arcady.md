## Price rule outcome

**Price does NOT go in the Direct ad, or in any Arcady ad.**

- The lowest "from" price on any source is **$1,418,000** (1BR + Study, 527 sqft, [NL], 04-Nov-2026). That is $18,000 over the $1.4M cap.
- The developer's own price page ([ARC], 05-Nov-2026) shows the 1BR + Study **fully sold**. Its cheapest available unit is the **2BR at $1,945,000**.
- On either source the entry is above $1.4M, so the rule applies: no price in the headline, subhead or fine print. The Direct ad leads with the top USP instead (rank 1 below).
- The "$1.33M" entry in `copy.md` (AR-1 to AR-5) and the agent's June "From $1.4M" headline are **out of date**. No current source supports either figure.

## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations (what the data shows):**
- "Freehold" is in about 14 of the 37 headlines, including our own. One advertiser (Choice Property Guru) runs 9 near-identical freehold-vs-leasehold ads. [raw pull]
- Three differently named pages ran the identical school headline within one week in October. [raw pull]
- No ad in the pull states an MRT walk time in minutes, an Orchard/CBD drive time, facilities, specs, orientation, or the Bendemeer food centre. [raw pull]
- The "From $1.4M" ads ran in June 2026. As of 4–5 Nov 2026 the 1BR + Study is either down to 1 unit at $1.418M [NL] or sold out [ARC]. [NL], [ARC]
- Three differently numbered Facebook pages named "The Arcady" / "The Arcady at Boon Keng" ran a catalog (2024), e-brochure + "updated offers" (Aug 2026) and a "VVIP Priority Pass" (Jul 2026). [raw pull]. [ARC] says its sales reps are paid by the developer. [ARC] /price/
- [NL] lists 5 units as "unreleased". [NL]
- Sources disagree on MRT walk time (6 vs 7 min), TOP (Q4 2027 vs Dec 2028), Bendemeer Primary distance (170m vs 0.6km) and stock per type. [ARC], [EP], [PN], [NL]
- Creative bodies, visuals and CTAs could not be seen (Facebook 403).

**Interpretations (our read on why, and what to do about it):**
- **Saturated:** freehold as the headline (fine as a subhead, per the client rule "rare freehold condo", but not a differentiator); the freehold-vs-leasehold comparison (Choice Property Guru owns it at 9 ads); the Hong Wen "within 1KM" hook (3 pages, same week); e-brochure/price-list lead magnets.
- **Dead:** price. It was used once in June and is now invalid under the $1.4M rule.
- **White space GoAhead can own:**
  1. A *numbered* MRT claim ("6-min walk to Boon Keng MRT", 3 stops to Dhoby Ghaut).
  2. City-fringe drive times (ION Orchard 11 min).
  3. Resort facilities and the 4 landscaped levels.
  4. Build and spec quality (north/south facing, 2.85m ceilings, brand fittings).
  5. The Bendemeer food-centre neighbourhood.
- The pages named "The Arcady" are probably developer-appointed marketing agencies, since the catalog, "updated offers" and VVIP mechanics are typical. That is unverified. If they are the developer's, GoAhead's Direct ad competes with them for name-aware searchers, as Amber House did with Far East.
- The July "VVIP Priority Pass" ran 2.5 years after launch. It may tie to a price release or to the 5 unreleased units. That is a guess, but if true, a future release could change the entry price. Re-check price before every wave.
- About 30% of stock is left, so the remaining buyer pool is bigger than Amber House's (~15%). Expect CPL closer to the account norm than to Amber House's ~S$70. That is untested.
- Without a price, the creative doesn't pre-qualify on budget. As with Amber House, the lead form's budget question does the filtering. The cheapest available unit is ~$1.95M (if the 1BR is gone), so the cut-off band should sit around S$1.9M.
