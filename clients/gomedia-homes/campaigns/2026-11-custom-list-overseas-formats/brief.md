## Short answer to the user's question

**Yes. The Dubai formats (ref-04/05/06) fit the New Launch Custom List of Homes offer directly.** They sell a multi-project catalogue, not one building, which is exactly our offer. The Melbourne formats (ref-01/02/03) are single-project layouts. They can also be used inside the custom list as layout shells (giant number, info card, price box + checklist), as long as no condo is named. As a secondary track, they slot into Direct new-launch project ads (see the end of this brief).

What we can't copy from them: the rental guarantee, rental income, "strong investment potential", unsourced "save up to" savings, "Leading Developers" (unless true), and any price that hasn't been confirmed. Their text and images are format references only.

**Where the data agrees with the lead's read, and where I push back:**
- Agreed: the custom list is the main fit. The bedroom + price headline is saturated (8+ competitors, see the competitor file), so the test is about new mechanics, not another "N-bedder from $X" headline.
- Pushback 1, **funnel:** the cheapest GoAhead result (affordability angle, S$17.79/lead, n=118) ran on a **website** path ("Landing Page Type 2"). The instant-form preference comes from the Arcady and Hougang website campaigns failing. I still recommend instant forms for every cell (2 projects out of 3 say so, and it keeps the cells comparable), but the S$17.79 number is not a like-for-like benchmark for a form.
- Pushback 2, **the "EC" card in the Dubai 3-card row:** in this account EC leads cost ~S$43–55, about 2x condo (learnings 2026-11-05), and need an eligibility question. Default cards = **by bedroom tier**. Only include an EC tier if the user wants EC buyers in this list, with "EC eligibility conditions apply."
- Pushback 3, **"in 1 minute" has to be true.** It refers to filling in the form (cut to 3 questions, see Conversion path). The list itself is sent later on WhatsApp, so copy must not imply the list arrives in 1 minute unless the client confirms it does.
- Pushback 4, **too many mechanics for one test.** Six overseas layouts x 3 angles is too many cells for this account. Layouts become assets inside 3 angle cells (see test-plan.md). The layout read will be directional only.

## Goal
Generate new-launch buyer leads for the **New Launch Custom List of Homes** offer (a personal list of Singapore new launches matched to budget and bedroom count) at or under the account's alert target, and learn whether the overseas "personal catalogue" mechanics beat our current bedroom + price list ads.

## KPI
- **Primary:** cost per instant-form lead **<= S$25** (account alert target for GoAhead buyer; condo benchmark S$22–31 from Arcady/Hougang in learnings).
- **Guardrail:** qualified-lead rate from sales tags not worse than the control cell. *Qualified lead is still TODO in brand.md.* Proposed definition for the user to confirm: budget band matches at least one listed project + buying timeline <= 12 months (asked on WhatsApp) + not a licensed agent + replies on WhatsApp within 48h. Who tags the leads, where, and with which tags is set out under "Lead-quality tagging" below. **Without tagging, any result is CPL-only and low confidence.**
- Budget: **not given.** test-plan.md carries a proposal that the user has to confirm.

## ICP segment
**HDB upgraders and first-time private buyers who know their budget but not which launch fits it** (brand.md segment "HDB upgraders": pain = affordability and timing, want = low monthly payment and progressive payment).
Excluded on purpose: investors. brand.md lists their want as "returns / capital growth", which we can't claim (banned claims, section 5). EC buyers only if the user opts in (pushback 2).
Targeting: Meta Housing special ad category, so Singapore broad, no age/gender/postcode targeting. Eligibility and budget live in the form, not the targeting.

## Awareness stage
**Solution-aware.** They know they want a new launch condo. They don't know which of this month's launches fit their budget and bedroom count, and they don't know GoAhead.

## Objection to overcome
"There are too many launches and price lists. I can't tell which ones I can actually afford, and leaving my number means agent spam."
The ad answers the first half (a personal custom list by budget). The form and thank-you screen answer the second half: say what they'll get, when and how.

## How the three angles stay different (all three now show prices)
C1 and C2 both show bedroom prices, so the test isolates **what the ad leads with**, not whether a price is shown:
- **C1 leads with the promise** (speed + a list made for you). The hero is the headline "Your custom list of homes in 1 minute". The bedroom ladder is small supporting proof under it: **floor prices only ("from"), one line per bedroom, no ceilings, never the biggest element.**
- **C2 leads with the price range.** The hero is a number or a price box (ceiling + floor, giant number). **No speed or "made for you" promise in the headline or chips**, just a plain "Get the custom list" CTA.
- **C3 leads with the monthly figure.** No bedroom ladder, no ceiling; one monthly number with its disclaimer.

To keep the read clean, the floor prices are the same confirmed numbers in every cell that shows them, and C2's floors match C1's ladder.

## Angles (max 3)

### Angle 1 — "Your custom list of homes in 1 minute" (speed + personalisation, price ladder as support)
- **Angle in one sentence:** "Tell us your bedrooms and budget in a 1-minute form, and we'll send you a custom list of only the new launches that fit."
- **Hypothesis:** If the hero is the speed + personal-list promise (with the bedroom ladder only as support), solution-aware buyers will fill in the form at <= S$25 and at a lower CPL than C2's price-led hero, because the promise looks different from the 8+ competitors leading with bedroom + price, while the ladder still answers "is this in my range?".
- **Proof:** (a) The offer exists: the client's own custom-list ads (`new-launch-custom-list/ref-06`, DOWNLOAD LIST). (b) Market signal, not proof of results: Metropolitan Premium Properties runs the same catalogue promise in story and feed carousel versions (ref-04, ref-06). (c) Saturation data: competitor file 2026-11-04, 8+ pages on bedroom + price. (d) "1 minute" is true because the form is cut to 3 questions (Conversion path). (e) Trust chips "No Duplicate / Real-Time Listing / No Fake Listing" come from the client's own ref-06. **The client must confirm they're still true** before reuse.
- **Headline:** "Your custom list of homes in 1 minute" or "Get your custom list of homes in 1 minute". Chips: "Free custom list" + one trust chip ("Developer price lists" or a confirmed ref-06 chip).
- **Bedroom price ladder (on every C1 asset, placeholders until the client gives prices):**
  `1-bedder from $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]` / `2-bedder from $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]` / `3-bedder from $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]` / `4-bedder from $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]` + the price disclaimer.
- **Formats to use:** ref-04 story layout (headline, 2 pill chips, the ladder in place of the single "Starting from" tab, 3 tilted cards = bedroom tiers), ref-06 carousel (card 1 = the 1-minute custom list promise, cards 2–5 = one bedroom tier each with its "from" price, **no project names**), ref-02 cream info card with the ladder inside the card and a round "Free custom list" seal (never a savings or discount seal). Every asset ships in **9:16 and 4:5** (see test-plan.md).
- **C1 now shows prices, so it waits for confirmed prices too.** A price-free C1 version (ladder removed) is kept **only as a fallback** for the optional pre-test in test-plan.md. It's never part of the main read.
- **Hook directions (direction, not final copy):** speed promise ("Your custom list of homes in 1 minute") / identity call-out ("Upgrading from HDB? Get your custom list") / pain ("Too many launches? Get a custom list of only the ones in your budget").

### Angle 2 — "Know the real range first" (budget range, price as the hero)
- **Angle in one sentence:** "See what a new launch actually costs at your bedroom count, from the cheapest entry up to your ceiling."
- **Hypothesis:** If the hero is the price range itself (ceiling + floor), buyers decide "can I afford it?" before the click, so C2 gets a **higher qualified-lead rate** (more `real buyer – reachable`, fewer budget mismatches) than C1, at a similar or slightly higher CPL.
- **Proof:** Price anchors are the most common hook in this market (competitor file), which is why the *layout* changes and not the claim: ref-05 dual anchor (ceiling in the headline + floor in a box), ref-01 giant number, ref-03 outlined price box + tick checklist. **Every price is missing and has to come from the client** (see "Numbers we need").
- **Checklist lines allowed under the price box (ref-03 style):** "Developer price lists, no guesswork" / "Matched to your budget and bedroom count" / "Floor plans included" / "Expert advice you can trust" (existing GoAhead trust footer) / "Progressive payment, pay as it's built" (plain fact, no figures unless sourced). **Never** rental guarantee, rental income, returns, "investment potential". No "1 minute" or speed line in C2.
- **Price placeholders the copywriter must keep as-is until filled:**
  - Floor: `from $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]` (same figure as the C1 ladder for that bedroom count)
  - Ceiling per tier: `[N]-bedders under $[PRICE — client to confirm, source]`

### Angle 3 — "Pay as it's built" (monthly / progressive payment)
- **Angle in one sentence:** "A new launch can cost less per month than you think during construction. Get the custom list that fits your monthly budget."
- **Hypothesis:** The affordability angle was the cheapest GoAhead ad at S$17.79/lead (n=118, learnings 2026-11-03). Putting a monthly figure in the ref-03 price box / ref-01 giant number layout should bring CPL under S$25 on an instant form, below both C1 and C2.
- **Proof:** learnings.md row 2026-11-03 (S$17.79, medium confidence, website path). brand.md offer "From $400/month progressive payment" list, assumption: 30-year loan at 3.5%, phase 1 progressive payment, disclaimer required. **The "$400/month" figure is only usable if the client confirms which unit price it was calculated on and that it's still valid this month.** Otherwise use `from $[MONTHLY — client to confirm, loan assumptions, source]`.
- **Required disclaimer on every C3 asset (in the ad image, fine print and primary text), all blanks filled from client data:**
  "*Illustration only. Based on a $[UNIT PRICE] unit at the [STAGE, e.g. first] progressive payment stage, [TENURE]-yr loan at [RATE]% interest, [LTV]% loan-to-value. Monthly instalments increase as construction progresses. Excludes stamp duties and fees. Not a loan offer.*"
  A monthly figure without the stage named and the "instalments increase as construction progresses" line fails QA.
- **Must remove:** the old ad's returns claim ("20–30% gains"), as flagged in brand.md and learnings.

## Conversion path
Ad (static / carousel, every asset in 9:16 Stories/Reels + 4:5 Feed) -> **Meta instant form, higher-intent type, 3 short questions** -> thank-you screen -> GoAhead consultant sends the custom list on WhatsApp and asks the follow-up questions.
- CTA button: **Sign up** or **Download** (Meta button). On-image CTA: **GET MY CUSTOM LIST** / **DOWNLOAD LIST**. Not "GET QUOTE": the reader asks for a list, not a consultation (static-ad-rules CTA lesson).
- **Form (same for all cells, so "1 minute" is honest):**
  1. Bedrooms (1 / 2 / 3 / 4+)
  2. Budget band
  3. "Are you a licensed property agent?"
  Plus Meta's prefilled name + phone. If the user opts EC in: one EC eligibility question that doesn't ask income or citizenship (still well under a minute). QA/tracking check each question is allowed under the Housing category.
- **Moved to the WhatsApp follow-up:** buying timeline, own stay or investment. The consultant asks these when sending the custom list and logs the answers in the tagging sheet.
- Thank-you screen: "Thanks! Your custom list of homes will be sent on WhatsApp within [TIME — client to confirm]." No VIP/preview promises.
- WhatsApp first message (direction): "Hi [name], here's your custom list of homes for [bedrooms] within [budget band]. ..." then the timeline and own-stay questions.
- No landing page in this campaign. Website path is out (learnings: Arcady and Hougang website campaigns failed).

## Lead-quality tagging (must be in place before launch)
The quality guardrail, the lead-quality kill and the day-30 read all depend on this. **User question, not yet answered:**

- **Where:** [the lead sheet or CRM the instant-form leads land in, client to confirm], one row per lead with the ad set name (C0–C3) kept, plus the timeline and own-stay answers from WhatsApp.
- **How often:** within 48h of each lead; performance pulls the tags on day 7, day 14 and day 30.
- **Tags (one per lead):** `real buyer – reachable` · `real buyer – budget doesn't match` · `agent` · `fake / wrong number` · `unreachable after 3 tries`. Optional second tag: `qualified` per the definition under KPI.
- **If nobody is set up to tag by launch day:** the test still runs, but every verdict is **CPL-only, low confidence**, the lead-quality kill can't fire, and no cell is scaled past the first +50% step until tags exist. Tracking checks the setup before launch.

## Must / must-not
- **Must:**
  - Call the offer "Custom List of Homes" / "custom list" everywhere.
  - Every price and monthly figure carries a source and a disclaimer, e.g. "*Prices from developer price lists as at [DATE]. Subject to availability and change. Excludes stamp duties.*" Monthly figures carry the full C3 disclaimer above.
  - "*IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY*" on staged/stock interiors. AI or stock building images get "*IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development*".
  - Real interior photo collage feel. Red house colour by default. A blue variant (Dubai ref-04/05 palette) is allowed as the format nod.
  - Housing special ad category. Advantage+ creative enhancements **off** in test cells.
  - CEA licence/registration line if required: still TODO in brand.md, client to confirm.
  - Every asset in 9:16 and 4:5. Story versions keep text out of the top and bottom Meta UI zones. Preview in-placement before export.
- **Must not:**
  - Any other name for the list (the boss team rejected the earlier C1 wording, 2026-11-07).
  - Rental guarantees, rental income, returns, gains, "investment potential", "capital growth", "guaranteed".
  - Savings / discount / stamp-duty / free-gift badges unless a real, sourced developer offer exists. For a multi-project list that will almost never apply.
  - Unit counts, "% sold", district codes (D15 etc.), street addresses.
  - Project names in this campaign. It's a list offer, and naming a condo turns it into a Direct project ad with its own rules.
  - "Leading Developers" chip unless the list really is limited to named major developers. Use "Developer price lists" instead.
  - Invented prices, "1 minute" used for delivery of the list (unless confirmed), urgency ("last chance") that isn't true. No reused price from an old ad without a fresh source.
  - Copying any overseas ad's text, images, logos or offers.
