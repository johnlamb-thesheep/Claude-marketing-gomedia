The Alt VO is 44 words. The end-card fine print is unchanged, because the cards still show prices.

**Choreography (what leads, what follows):** one thing moves at a time. The headline leads beats 1–2, then yields (it shrinks) before the sub line arrives. The chips follow the sub line. The price box leads the cards (anchor first, range second). The cards are the only motion in beat 6. The CTA is the last and only moving element at the end. Entrances are ease-out and there's no bouncy easing on the price or the fine print.

**Safe zones:** no text in the top ~270 px (14%) or the bottom ~380 px (20%), except the caption pill. **The user's static breaks both** (eyebrow at y≈190, CTA pill at y≈1690), so the video layout moves the eyebrow down to y ≥ 290 and puts the CTA pill at y≈1120–1250. Captions stay in y≈1420–1490 in every beat.

**Length check:** 45 raw words ≈ 18 s raw → ≈ 15.0 s at 1.2x, + 0.6 s hold ≈ 15.6 s. If the real read lands above ~15.8 s, use 1.25x and tell the client which speed was used (GUIDELINES §2).

## End-card spec (13.3–15.6 s)

- **Background:** the static's light-blue gradient. The 4 cards stay in place (opacity 0.85) with the `*IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual unit*` label.
- **Line above the CTA (Montserrat 700, ~40 px, dark navy):** "3 questions · about 1 minute · sent on WhatsApp within [TIME — client to confirm]"
- **Layout:** the cards shrink to 80% and move up to y≈700–1040, which frees the lower half.
- **CTA pill:** white pill, black condensed caps "GET MY CUSTOM LIST", centred, ~760×130 px, at y≈1120–1250. Pulse 1.00→1.04, period 0.8 s.
- **Caption pill:** y≈1420–1490 (above y 1500).
- **Fine print (≥ 28 px at 1080 width, held ≥ 2 s, left-aligned, y≈1280–1400, above the captions):**
  - `*Prices from developer price lists as at [DATE]. Subject to availability and change. Excludes stamp duties.`
  - `[CEA LICENCE / REGISTRATION LINE — client to confirm if required]`
- **Logo:** optional GoAhead logo top-right (y ≥ 290), supplied by the client. Never generated.
- **Never ship brackets.** Every `[ ]` is filled or the line is removed before render.

## Music mood

Bright, light, optimistic minimal pop/house (soft plucks or marimba, light claps), ~110–118 BPM, no vocals, no drops. Ducked about −18 dB under the VO, swelling slightly in the 0.6 s end-card hold. Royalty-free/licensed track only (Meta Sound Collection or a library the client licenses). Note: house guidelines default to **no music** ("voice + 2–3 small effects"), so render a music-off version too and let the client pick. If music is used, keep the SFX to the thud, the pops and the ding, and drop the rest.
