## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- Families and upgraders (HDB or condo) who need 3 to 5 bedrooms and want a quieter, greener Holland address. Budget S$2.5M and up, so a narrow, high-budget pool.
- Many will have school-age children, and a good share drive (the MRT isn't walkable today).
- Entry is $2.542M, far above the ~$1.6M line, so **no price anywhere**. The lead form does the budget filtering.

**The facts we highlight, and why**
1. **Low-rise homes, only 4 to 6 storeys, beside the Rail Corridor in a new green Holland precinct** (USP 1). The most distinctive thing about the project, and nobody is advertising it.
2. **Family-sized homes only: 3 to 5 bedrooms, 872 to 1,572 sqft, with 3.18m ceilings and Flexi / Study options** (USP 3). Tells the right buyer straight away that it's for them.
3. **Methodist Girls' School (Primary) about 770–780m away** (USP 2). The emotional family hook, always written as "near" with the MOE line.
4. **Wellness and resort facilities: 50m and 30m lap pools, a kids' Discovery Pool, a thermal outdoor bath, a red light therapy room** (USP 4). Replaces the Price slot.
5. **The first condo in Holland Plain, by Sim Lian Group** (USP 5). Direct ad only, with no "first-mover" or growth framing.

**What competitors already say (so we stand apart)**
- Almost nothing. The only ads found are ~30 copies from one Vietnamese-currency page ("A First Look at Amberwood at Holland / 4.9 / 5.0 stars"). No developer or Singapore agent ads surfaced.
- So every angle is open. We take the family/school and low-rise green-living story, which nobody runs. We never copy the "4.9 / 5.0 stars" line (unsourced).

**What we must NOT say**
- Any price, psf or monthly figure, and any discount talk (the NL price looks slightly lower than launch, but that's unverified).
- MRT walk minutes, and "near King Albert Park MRT" on any image. Stacked's "~9-minute walk" depends on the future Community Plain being built, and its pricing review says "no MRT nearby". King Albert Park MRT appears in primary text only, as "in the area" (perf AW2).
- The Cross Island Line as open. It's only mentioned with "planned, target 2032; subject to change", in A4's primary text.
- "Red light therapy, first in Singapore". I've left the "first" claim out entirely.
- "First-mover advantage", "growth", "potential". Green Mark as achieved. The childcare centre as operating.
- Unit counts (including "boutique"), % sold, "D10", street names (no "Holland Link", no "Holland Link Road"), the columbarium (sales objection only).
- Any school admission implication. No single school in a headline.

- Word counts: headline ≤8, subhead ≤14 ("5-Bedroom", "Low-Rise", "low-rise", "3.18m", "50m", "Kids'", "Girls'" and "&" count as one word each).
