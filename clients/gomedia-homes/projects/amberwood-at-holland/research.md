> **Headline findings (read first)**
> 1. **Entry price is $2.542M (3BR, 872 sqft)**, per newlaunches.sg updated 07-Nov-2026 [NL]. The launch figure was "from $2.568M" [SH-REV], [SH-PR]. That is **far above the ~$1.6M cap, so no price goes in any ad.** The Price slot must be replaced.
> 2. **This is a family-only product: 3, 4 and 5 bedrooms, 872–1,572 sqft, with no 1BR or 2BR** [SH-REV]. That narrows the audience to upgraders and families with a $2.5M+ budget.
> 3. **The MRT is not walkable today.** King Albert Park MRT is ~1.0km straight-line [OM]. Stacked says "~9-minute walk" **only once the future Community Plain is completed** [SH-REV], and its pricing review says "no MRT nearby" [SH-PR]. **No MRT walk-time ad.**
> 4. **The hot USPs are low-rise living in a new green precinct, Methodist Girls' School under 1km, 3.18m ceilings and wellness facilities.**

## 6. Objections / risks

1. **Holland Plain is mostly a construction site today.** Few amenities, and you need to drive or bus for errands [SH-SLOW], [SH-REV].
2. **No MRT within walking distance yet**; the CRL interchange comes after TOP (2032) [SH-REV], [SH-PR].
3. **Prime price without a complete lifestyle.** Entry ~$2.5M; psf above most nearby resale (mostly < $2,400 psf) [SH-PR], [SH-SLOW].
4. **A columbarium sits across Holland Link Road** (Stacked notes the layout mitigates it) [SH-REV].
5. Layout nitpicks: small common bedrooms (~97 sqft), some showflats remove walls, short kitchen counters in the 3BR Flexi [SH-REV].
6. Years of nearby construction; future supply of 8 projects in the precinct [SH-REV].

## 8. Observations vs interpretations

**Observations:**
- Entry $2.542M [NL].
- ~16% sold with 178 left [NL].
- 24 sales in Sept [SH-SEP].
- Stacked ran a "harder to sell" piece on 4 Oct [SH-SLOW].
- Only one overseas page advertises it [raw].

**Interpretations:**
- This is the hardest of the four for lead-gen economics: no price lead, no walkable MRT, a family-only quantum of $2.5M+.
- The ads should sell the lifestyle (low-rise, green, schools, space) and let the form filter the budget (S$2.5M+ band).
- Recommend performance sets a tight stop-loss.
