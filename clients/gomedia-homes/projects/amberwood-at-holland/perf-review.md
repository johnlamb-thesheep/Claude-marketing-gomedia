### Rulings asked for

1. **"Near King Albert Park MRT" (A1, A4, A5 subheads): swap it out.** Use the copywriter's Flag 3 swaps.
   - Why: ~1.0km straight-line with no walking route verified today; the only walk figure depends on unbuilt infrastructure; and Stacked's pricing review says "no MRT nearby" in print — a buyer who checks finds a published contradiction. Coastal Cabana's "near" at ~0.9km was already "at the edge"; this is past it, with a contrary source. For a S$2.5M+ family buyer who mostly drives, the MRT isn't the selling point, and putting it on 3 of 5 images invites commuter leads who'll be disappointed.
   - Keep "King Albert Park MRT on the Downtown Line is in the area" in the A1/A4/A5 **primary text** (true, not a proximity promise), and the hedged CRL line in A4.
2. **A4 place labels moved off the photo into a chip row: Approved.** Correct call — it stops a generic trail being captioned as the real Rail Corridor. With ruling 1, drop the `KING ALBERT PARK MRT` chip, leaving `RAIL CORRIDOR` · `BUKIT TIMAH NATURE RESERVE` (AW3).
3. **Floor-plan ad with no building image (A3):** OK for draft images, same as Coastal Cabana R2-B. Needs the user's OK for this project. The fallback title `3-BEDROOM LAYOUT · ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT TO SCALE` (no size on the sheet) is right.
4. **Lead-form price line: recommend yes, the user decides.** This project has the biggest gap between the ad (no price) and the entry ($2.54M), so it's where a price line saves the most wasted calls. If allowed: `3-bedroom from about S$2.54M (as at [date]); subject to availability.` on the intro screen only. Expect a higher CPL and a much better cost per qualified lead.
