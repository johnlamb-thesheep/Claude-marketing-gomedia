## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- Own-stay families and upgraders with a **$2.3M+ budget** who want freehold in a central, convenient area (the cheapest unit is a 2BR + Study at ~$2.27M).
- Families with school-age children looking at the Novena school belt.
- Buyers who value a big-name developer (Far East Organization) and a full set of facilities in a smaller building.
- Message direction only. No targeting by age, family status or income (Housing special ad category).

**Facts we highlight, and why**
1. **Rare freehold condo in Novena.** #1 USP and a client rule: "rare freehold condo" on every ad. Huttons: only ~7% of new CCR non-landed homes launched 1Q2024–2Q2026 were freehold. [SHPV], [SHR]
2. **8-minute walk to Novena MRT (North-South Line)**, the developer's figure as reported by Stacked. A real walk-time fact, so we state it directly. [SHR], [OM]
3. **Malls nearby:** Velocity and Square 2 (next to Novena MRT), Zhongshan Mall, United Square. Straight-line only, so "near", never minutes. [NL], [SHU]
4. **Big-condo facilities in one tower:** a 50m cantilevered lap pool, a full-size tennis court, spa and kids' pools, gym, pet park, sky terrace. This replaces the Price slot. [SHPV], [SHR], [SHU]
5. **3-bedroom layouts from 764 sqft** (the deepest stock, 37 left) with 2.89m ceilings; St. Joseph's Institution Junior nearby. [SHPV], [SHR], [NL]

**Price rule outcome:** entry $2,266,264 (2BR + Study) is clearly above ~$1.6M, so **no price, psf or monthly figure anywhere**, including the Direct ad. **The Price slot is replaced by a Facilities ad (S5).**

**What competitors already say** ([AL], 4 relevant pages)
- **Far East Property Sales** (many ads since ~30 Oct): "New Freehold Chapter in Novena", "Freehold 3BR Units in Novena", "Freehold 4BR Units in Novena", "Freehold Homes in Novena".
- Two "The Serra Residences" pages: "Discover Serra Residences", "Register your interest for more new updates" (pre-launch wording).
- Ronald Lee: "New-Launch 3 BR CCR Condos under $3M" (multi-project price band).
- **So:** "freehold + Novena" is the developer's own drumbeat. Our required "rare freehold condo" line will look similar, so **every ad leads with a second fact** the developer isn't using: the 8-min walk + malls, the 50m pool + tennis court, the family/school hook, or the 3BR floor plan. Nobody runs MRT, malls, facilities, school or floor-plan angles for Serra.

**What we must NOT say**
- Any price, psf or monthly figure (including Stacked's ~$6,125/month illustration).
- "Only / last freehold in Novena", "first freehold in X years" (the 16 years is how long Far East held the land). "Rare" means rare among new launches, and stays exactly "rare freehold condo".
- Freehold appreciation stats (returns claim, banned by brand.md).
- Walk minutes to any mall. Views from specific floors. Developer discounts (unknown).
- Tan Tock Seng Hospital / HealthCity Novena, in copy or visuals.
- % sold, unit counts ("133-unit boutique"), "D11", "CCR", the street address (Bassein Road / Moulmein Road).
- "Register your interest" or preview wording (launched 16 Oct 2026).
- Fittings brand names (V-ZUG, Liebherr, etc.) are held back until QA clears brand-name use.
- 2 Moulmein Road lifestyle precinct (planned, tenant mix uncertain): held back, Flag 6.
- A school as the visual hero (badge or big label): perf SR2. The school appears in the subhead and primary text only.
