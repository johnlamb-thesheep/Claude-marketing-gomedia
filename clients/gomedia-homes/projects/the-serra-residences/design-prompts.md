## Rules baked into every prompt

- **Only S1 shows "The Serra Residences".** S2–S5 show no name, developer (Far East Organization), street (Bassein Road / Moulmein Road) or logo.
- **"Rare freehold condo" on every ad, on the image:** S1 headline, S2–S5 subheads. Never "only", "last" or "first freehold".
- **No price, "$", psf, monthly figure or discount anywhere.**
- No unit counts ("133-unit"), "% sold", "D11", "CCR", street names, register-interest or preview wording, fittings brand names, the 2 Moulmein Road precinct, the Summit / private-lift tier, views from specific floors.
- **No hospital (Tan Tock Seng / HealthCity Novena) in any frame, map or label.**
- Walk time: "8-min walk to Novena MRT" is the developer's figure and carries `Walk time per developer.` Malls are "near" only, no minutes. Full names "Novena MRT", "North-South Line". No real line colours (NSL red included), no LTA/MRT logos, no station codes.
- Schools: "near" only, the MOE line on the image where a school appears. No school buildings, uniforms or crests.
- **No AI-generated condo tower, facade or skyline.** No identifiable faces.
- Image labels (perf T10): generic photos `IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development` (interior standing in for a unit type: `– not the actual unit`). `IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY` only on an official render. Map: `Illustrative map, not to scale.` Plan: `ILLUSTRATIVE LAYOUT ONLY, not to scale`.
- Floor plan: generic line drawing with **no text, numbers, compass or scale bar inside it** until the official 3BR plan is supplied.
- ~60 px clear strip above every CTA for the CEA particulars. Brand constants: red `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER` eyebrow (#D0021B) and `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE` (Meta button **Download**).
- 9:16: no text in the top 14% or the bottom 20%.
