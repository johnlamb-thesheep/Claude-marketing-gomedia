## 5. Objections / risks to prepare for

1. **Next to Tan Tock Seng Hospital / HealthCity Novena.** Stacked: "a dealbreaker for some buyers" [SHR]. Don't show the hospital in visuals. Prepare an answer (convenience for care vs ambulance noise).
2. **Small homes for freehold money:** Stacked's headline "New Freehold, Smaller Homes". Older Novena freehold condos give more space per dollar [SHR], [SHPR].
3. **High psf:** avg ~$3,460 psf at launch. Stacked: "above most Novena resale averages" [SHPR], [GN-SR].
4. **Slow launch:** BT headline "sells less than a quarter of units released" [GN-SR]. Context only. Never mention sales.
5. **Building details:** 7 units per floor share 2 lifts in the lower tier; parking ~85%; some studies, kitchens and bathrooms have no windows; narrow roads at peak hours [SHR].
6. **2 Moulmein Road** tenant mix uncertain [SHR].
7. Stacked [SHU] says the area is "widely populated with other freehold but boutique apartments". **"Rare" means rare among new launches, not rare in Novena.** Keep the line as "rare freehold condo" (the client rule) and never claim "the only freehold in Novena".

## 6. Recommendations for the copywriter (data, not strategy)

**CTA:** "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE". Launched 16 Oct 2026. **Not "Register your interest"**, even though one page still uses it.

**Top 5 facts for the 5 angles** (price slot replaced)
1. **Direct:** "The Serra Residences: rare freehold condo in Novena by Far East Organization" + "8-min walk to Novena MRT". **No price.**
2. **Blind (need-led):** "Looking for a freehold home for your family?" → "Rare freehold condo in Novena, 2–5 bedroom homes from 710 sqft, near St. Joseph's Institution Junior." No name. Add the MOE line.
3. **Floor layout:** 3BR, 764–904 sqft, or 2BR + Study, 710 sqft (dumbbell). Real plan needed (rights TBC). "Rare freehold condo" in the subhead.
4. **MRT:** "8-min walk to Novena MRT, near Velocity, Square 2 & United Square". Fine print: "Walk time per developer; distances approx."
5. **Price slot → replace with "Resort facilities, boutique tower":** "A 50m lap pool and tennis court in a rare freehold condo in Novena." No price.

**Safe supporting lines:** "Expected TOP 4Q2030". "Private-lift homes on the upper floors". "Premium fittings by V-ZUG, Liebherr & Hansgrohe" (check the brand-name use with QA). "Upcoming lifestyle precinct at 2 Moulmein Road (target 2027)": hedge it, since the tenant mix is uncertain.

**UNVERIFIED / do not use**
- Any price, psf or monthly figure (rule).
- "Only / last freehold in Novena".
- "First freehold in X years". The "16 years" is how long Far East held the land, not a freehold drought.
- Freehold appreciation stats (Huttons' 36% vs 25%). These are investment-return claims and banned by brand.md.
- Developer discounts (unknown).
- Views from specific floors.
- Hospital adjacency as a positive in ads.
- Walk minutes to any mall (only straight-line distances exist).
- **Never:** % sold, unit counts ("133-unit boutique"), "D11", "CCR".

## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations:** freehold, launched 16 Oct 2026, entry $2.27M, ~20% sold at 07-Nov. Far East Property Sales is running many "Freehold … in Novena" ads; only 4 relevant pages in total. The most-transacted new launch in the 14 days to 28 Oct.

**Interpretations (my read; strategist to decide):** demand is real but the quantum is high, so expect higher CPL and a narrower buyer pool than the sub-$1.6M projects (this is an inference, not data). The freehold line on its own will blend with the developer's ads. The MRT/malls pair and the pool/tennis "big-condo facilities in a boutique tower" angle are open.
