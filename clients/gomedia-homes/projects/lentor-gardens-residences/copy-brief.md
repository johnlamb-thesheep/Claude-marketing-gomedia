## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is** (from launch reporting [SHL])
- Young couples buying a first home, and HDB upgraders from the Ang Mo Kio side. The 2-bedroom types hold the deepest stock (2BR Premium 29 left, 2BR + Study 100 left), so this is the core pool.
- Families wanting space and quiet (3BR/4BR stock is thinner, mostly 4BR Premium).
- Landed owners nearby right-sizing, and families wanting to live near parents.
- Message direction only. No targeting by age, family status or income (Housing special ad category).

**Facts we highlight, and why**
1. **About 200m of connected pools** (lagoon pool, 50m lap pool, kids' splash and play areas). #1 USP, and only a generic "resort-style living" line is used by anyone else. [SHR], [SHA]
2. **Beside Hillock Park, at the end of a quiet road.** Green, low-density, views over landed homes from selected units. Unused by competitors. [SHR]
3. **2-bedroom from $1.61M\*** (cheapest unit $1,607,900, 2BR Premium). Inside the "~$1.6M still OK" band, so Direct and Price lead with it. Points at real stock. [NL] 06-Nov-2026
4. **Family layouts and 2.9m ceilings:** 3BR/4BR with a foyer, separate wet and dry kitchens and a service yard. [SHR], [SHA]
5. **Near Lentor MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) and the Lentor Modern mall**, with the SLE and CTE for drivers. Said as "near" only. [OM], [NL], [SHR]

**What competitors already say** ([AL], 8 advertisers, 5 started 29 Oct–5 Nov: a busy auction)
- **Price:** "Lentor Gardens From $1.59M" (Steven Lin), "from $1,571,300" (Property Pa Pa), "From $2,292 PSF*" (a "Lentor Gardens Residences" page).
- **TOP:** "Expected TOP 2029" (2 agents).
- **Scarcity / 2BR:** "Limited Units Left", "Check 2-Bedroom Available Units".
- **MRT / resort:** "Near Lentor MRT. Connected Living.", "Come Home to Resort-Style Living".
- **Comparison is taken:** Singapore New Launch runs "4 Lentor Condos. Which Fits You?" and "Gardens Now or Signature Later?". We don't run a comparison ad here.
- **So we stand apart with:** the 200m pool as a specific number, Hillock Park / quiet road, a floor-plan hero, and an emotional family hook. Price stays, but each price ad carries a second fact nobody else uses.

**What we must NOT say**
- **TOP year** (2029 vs Dec 2030 conflict). Not on any ad.
- Walk minutes to Lentor MRT (none sourced, and Stacked says it's further than other Lentor condos). No "next to" or "steps from".
- "$2,292 psf" or any psf. "Side gate to Hillock Park" (approval pending). The rival "Signature". Kingsford track-record claims.
- The childcare centre (operator TBC). Schools by name in headlines (nearest are ~1.1–1.2km).
- "Limited units", % sold, unit counts (including the strata terraces count), "D26", the street name, "Lentor Hills growth" or any investment framing.
- "VIP preview" or preview wording (launched Jul 2026).
- Prices of other types (2BR + Study $1.74M and up are above the cap).

## 6. Flags — confirm before launch
1. **The price is at the edge of the cap.** NL's cheapest is $1,607,900; two agents advertise $1,571,300 and $1.59M. "$1.61M" is inside the client's "~$1.6M still OK" band, but **the user should confirm they're happy to show it.** If the developer's live list shows the entry clearly above ~$1.6M, drop price everywhere: L1 uses Headline B with no price block, and L5 is replaced (suggested swap: a pool-led lifestyle ad, "About 200m of Pools, Beside Hillock Park"). If the live list shows a lower entry (e.g. $1,571,300), update every "$1.61M\*" to the exact rounded-up figure.
2. **Unit size of the cheapest stack isn't known.** The 2BR Premium ranges 646–678 sqft and the NL psf implies the $1.6079M unit is ~678 sqft. So the price block says "2-BEDROOM PREMIUM", not a sqft figure. QA confirms the size against the live list before any sqft is printed next to the price.
3. **TOP conflict (2029 vs Dec 2030).** Not printed anywhere. Two competitors print "2029"; if the developer confirms a date, it can be added as a fact tile later.
4. **The MRT is this project's weaker fact** (straight-line ~420m; Stacked says further than other Lentor condos). L4 stays a location map, and performance has put it on hold (no pool swap).
5. **3BR stock is thin** (about 9 left across 3BR types; 4BR Premium 21). L2's family ad mentions 3 and 4-bedroom layouts; performance moved L2 to wave 2 after a stock check.
6. **CEA authorisation** (L1 Blocked if not). **Rights** to the official render and the 2BR + Study plan (L1, L3). A floor-plan ad without a building photo needs the user's OK in chat (perf ruling 2). The AI aerial pool on L1 is allowed with the generic label but should be replaced by the official facilities render once rights clear (perf ruling 4).
7. **Three "Lentor Gardens Residences" Facebook pages and lentorgardensresidences.com.sg** have unconfirmed ownership. Don't link them or reuse their assets.
8. **Busy auction:** 5 advertisers started in the last ~9 days, and two run price headlines close to ours. Performance expects S$30–50 per lead (low confidence). The read-only account pull is still to do.
