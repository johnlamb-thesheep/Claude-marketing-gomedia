## Rules baked into every prompt

- **Only L1 shows "Lentor Gardens Residences".** L2–L5 show no name, developer (Kingsford), street or logo, in text or image.
- Price only on L1 and L5: `$1.61M*` (entry $1,607,900, inside the client's "~$1.6M still OK" band, copy Flag 1). No psf (the competitors' "$2,292 PSF" included), discount or other unit-type prices.
- **No TOP year anywhere** (2029 vs Dec 2030 conflict, copy Flag 3).
- No unit counts, block or storey counts, "% sold", "Limited units", "D26", the street name, growth/investment framing, preview/VIP wording, childcare centre, "side gate to Hillock Park".
- MRT is "near" only: no "next to", "steps from", walk minutes or metres. Full names "Lentor MRT", "Thomson-East Coast Line". No real line colours (TEL brown included), no LTA/MRT logos, no station codes.
- "200m" of pools always carries `Pool length approx., per published review.` in the fine print.
- **No AI-generated condo tower, facade or skyline.** No identifiable faces. No school buildings, uniforms or crests.
- Image labels (perf T10): generic photos `IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development` (interiors standing in for a unit type: `– not the actual unit`). `IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY` only on an official render. Map: `Illustrative map, not to scale.` Plan: `ILLUSTRATIVE LAYOUT ONLY, not to scale`.
- Floor plan: generic line drawing with **no text, numbers or dimension figures inside it** until the official 2BR + Study plan is supplied.
- ~60 px clear strip above every CTA for the CEA particulars. Brand constants: red `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER` eyebrow (#D0021B) and `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE` (Meta button **Download**).
- 9:16: no text in the top 14% or the bottom 20%.
