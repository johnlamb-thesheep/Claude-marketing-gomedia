## 5. Objections / risks to prepare for

1. **Further from Lentor MRT than other Lentor condos** [SHR], [SHP]. Don't lead with walk time.
2. **High psf for Lentor:** priced above every Lentor launch except Lentor Modern on psf [SHP].
3. **Supply and competition in the estate:** it is the 7th new Lentor condo [SHL]. The GuocoLand-led **Lentor Central** site went at a record $1,278 psf ppr (Mar 2026) [GN-LG], [SHA]. The competitor ad "Gardens Now or Signature Later?" refers to an upcoming rival (name UNVERIFIED). Stacked notes rising supply may concern investors [SHR].
4. **Layout cons:** the 2BR kitchen has no window; the 4BR common bathroom has no window; corridor space in 3BR/4BR [SHR].
5. **Pool layout:** long walk-around, maintenance of the connected pools, and supervision of children [SHR].
6. **TOP date conflict** (2029 vs Dec 2030). Buyers will ask.
7. **Developer familiarity:** Kingsford is less known than the big local developers. Track record not checked in this pass.

## 6. Recommendations for the copywriter (data, not strategy)

**CTA:** "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE". Launched Jul 2026. **No "VIP preview" wording**, even though the developer page used it pre-launch.

**Top 5 facts for the 5 angles**
1. **Direct:** "Lentor Gardens Residences" + "2-bedroom from approx. $1.6M*" + "~200m pool & resort facilities". Fine print: "*Price as at 6 Nov 2026, subject to availability and change." **Recheck the price before launch.**
2. **Blind (need-led):** "Looking for a family home with room to grow?" → "New condo in Lentor: 3 & 4-bedroom layouts with wet & dry kitchens, ~200m pool, beside Hillock Park." No name.
3. **Floor layout:** the 2BR + Study, 732 sqft (most stock, 100 left: dumbbell, enclosable kitchen, 2.9m ceilings), or the 4BR Premium, 1,346 sqft (21 left). Real plan needed (rights TBC).
4. **MRT:** "Near Lentor MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) & Lentor Modern mall". **No minutes** (no source gives them, and the project is known to be further away).
5. **Price:** "2-bedroom homes in Lentor from approx. $1.6M*" (no name). Or, if price fails the recheck, use "Homes from 646 to 1,356 sqft in Lentor".

**Safe supporting lines:** "2.9m ceilings". "Childcare centre within the development (operator TBC)". Use with care, since no operator is confirmed. "Near CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls' School & Anderson Primary School" plus the MOE line. "Beside Hillock Park".

**UNVERIFIED / do not use**
- Walk minutes to Lentor MRT.
- TOP year (2029 vs Dec 2030 conflict).
- "$2,292 psf" (a competitor figure below the current lowest psf).
- "Side gate to Hillock Park" (approval pending).
- The rival project name "Signature".
- Kingsford track-record claims.
- **Never:** % sold, "limited units", unit counts, "D26", "Lentor Hills estate growth" or investment framing (e.g. CNA's sponsored "positioned for Lentor's next phase of growth").
