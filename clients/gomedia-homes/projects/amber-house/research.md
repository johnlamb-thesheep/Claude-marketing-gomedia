## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations (what the data shows):**
- Far East changes its Amber House headline roughly every 6 weeks: District 15 (Aug 20–21) → 3-bedroom + D15 (Aug 20) → 4-bedroom (Oct 1) → near 4 primary schools (Nov 4). Each theme runs as 3–4 parallel ads. [raw pull file]
- Every developer headline starts with "Freehold". None mentions price, psf, MRT, sales momentum, East Coast lifestyle, or the 2-bedroom. [raw pull file]
- The official site now headlines "Final 2 Three-Bedroom Homes" and marks two unit types FULLY SOLD. EdgeProp shows 84.8% sold. [OFF], [EP]
- The developer's "4 primary schools" matches the 4 starred schools on its locale page. Only one of them (Tanjong Katong Primary) is listed as a walk. [OFF] the-locale
- Current official per-type psf is S$3,055–S$3,308, which is above the launch-era "about S$2,900 psf". [OFF], BT headline
- Besides our own ad, one possible agent ad ("Rare Freehold Near MRT", Home Haven Hub) is live. No other agent ads naming Amber House were found. [raw pull file]
- Creative bodies, visuals and CTAs could not be seen (Facebook 403).

**Interpretations (our read on why, and what to do about it):**
- The rotation looks like inventory-led selling. The developer pushed 3BR in August, and 3BR is now down to the "final 2". It moved to 4BR (the biggest-ticket remaining stock, S$5.33M+) in October, then to a family/school hook in November that suits 3BR/4BR buyers. This is inference from timing only.
- The developer owns "freehold" + bedroom + school. Copying those adds little. Its headlines leave five openings GoAhead can own:
  1. **Price / quantum.** The developer never shows a number in its headlines. "From S$2.1M" for a freehold condo is a strong, sourced hook, and the client rules say price is the main selling point.
  2. **MRT walk time.** "4-min walk to Tanjong Katong MRT" is sourced and unused by the developer. Only Home Haven Hub is near it, with no number.
  3. **Scarcity / momentum.** "Over 80% sold" and two types sold out are real facts the developer isn't putting in headlines.
  4. **The 2-bedroom entry.** The developer has never headlined it, and it is the lowest quantum.
  5. **East Coast lifestyle.** Parkway Parade, i12 and Katong V are all a walk away, and East Coast Park is a short drive.
- The ad market for this project is thin (developer plus about 1 agent). GoAhead's Blind/MRT/Price ads face little direct overlap. Low competitor ad volume may also mean low remaining agent interest at ~85% sold. That is untested.
- With ~85% sold, the "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE" CTA still fits (a price list exists). The strategist should consider an availability-led offer (for example, a list of remaining units) and should confirm with the client what GoAhead can actually send.

## Recommended selling points for GoAhead (ranked)

These are inputs for the strategist, not a brief.

1. **Rare freehold at an entry price: "from S$2.1M" (2-bedroom).** CONFIRMED [OFF]. This leads the **Direct** ad, the only one that names the condo; per the client rules, Direct leads with price. Quantum is safer than psf because psf varies by type (S$3,055–S$3,308) and the old ~$2,950 figure is outdated. If psf is required, the only defensible form is "from approx. S$3,060 psf". That applies to the 4BR only, so quantum is cleaner. Example direction: "Amber House, Rare Freehold From $2.1M".
2. **4-min walk to Tanjong Katong MRT (TEL), between two MRT stations.** CONFIRMED (developer claim) [OFF]. Best non-name hook for the MRT and Blind ads, and the developer doesn't use it. Keep "5-min" if the client prefers a conservative figure.
3. **Scarcity backed by real facts: ~85% sold, two unit types sold out, final 3-bedroom homes.** CONFIRMED [EP], [OFF]. This is sourced urgency, which the skill rules allow. The figures move fast, so re-check on the day the ads go live and don't print a % older than a few days. Best as subhead or supporting proof.
4. **Boutique, 105 units, East Coast lifestyle: Parkway Parade and i12 Katong 9-min walk, East Coast Park 4-min drive.** CONFIRMED [OFF], [EP]. Works for Blind, Floor-layout and lifestyle subheads, and is a calmer alternative to the developer's school message. Use the area name only (no D15, no street address), per client rules.
5. **Family / schools: Tanjong Katong Primary 6-min walk; 4 primary schools nearby under the 2027 P1 Phase 2C two-track scheme.** CONFIRMED (developer framing) [OFF]. Ranked last because it's the developer's current headline and needs the MOE disclaimer. Never say "within 1km" (UNCONFIRMED). If used, prefer "6-min walk to Tanjong Katong Primary".

### Observations vs interpretations (round 2)

**Observations:**
- The developer's own story centres on **heritage design** (ADDP, columns, East Coast villas), **boutique freehold** and **the East Coast locale** [OFF-HOME], [OFF-INSP]. Its ads use none of the design story and lead with "Freehold" + bedroom + schools (round 1 table).
- The developer site gives **no** ceiling heights, efficiency figures, sea views or fittings brands. "Private lift access" is tied to "premium residences" only [OFF-HOUSE].
- Three of the four starred primary schools were balloted in 2025 Phase 2C. CHIJ (Katong) was not [SGS].
- Tanjong Katong MRT has been open since June 2024 [WK-TKT]. Some agent pages still say "upcoming" [PN].
- Agent sources disagree with the developer on the MRT walk (6 mins / ~430m vs 4 mins) [PN], [NLR], [OFF-LOC], and one agent source has the wrong building spec [NLR].
- An undated PropNex tower snapshot shows 2BR units (#16-04, #14-04, #13-04) and 4BR units (#13-03, #12-03, #11-03) unsold [PN]. Stock is for the lead form or consultant, not for ads.

**Interpretations:**
- With price and "% sold" both gone, **freehold + boutique + MRT** is the strongest factual core, and **family** and **Katong identity** are the strongest emotional hooks. Using different hooks per angle also meets the client rule that "every ad must look and sound different".
- The schools hook is the developer's current theme. GoAhead can still use it if it leads with the emotional question ("Looking for a home for your family?") rather than "near 4 primary schools", and uses the walkable school as proof.
- The heritage design story (U7) is the one thing no other condo can say. It fits the Direct ad, where the name is visible and the story can be told, better than a cold blind hook.
- Removing price removes the pre-qualifier that performance relied on for lead quality (perf-review C2-1, C5). The lead form will have to do that filtering (budget question). That is an observation for the strategist and landing-page stages, not a recommendation to show price.
- 2BR and 4BR stock (if PropNex's snapshot is current) points at two different buyers: smaller households/investors and big upgrader families. Hooks 2 and 4 suit the first group, and hooks 3 and 6 suit the second.
