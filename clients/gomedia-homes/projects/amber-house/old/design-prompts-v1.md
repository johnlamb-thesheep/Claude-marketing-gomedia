# Amber House — Image Prompts (v2)

5 angles. Revised 2026-11-04 to follow the new copy rules:

- Subheads sell advantages (rare freehold, minutes to MRT, bedrooms, price), not location codes. No "D15". The area name ("East Coast") is fine.
- Freehold is always called a "rare freehold condo".
- The **Direct** ad is the only one that names the condo, and it leads with price.
- The other 4 ads never name the condo, not in the headline, subhead or image.

**How to use:** copy the grey box under each angle and paste it into your image or video tool. Every prompt is complete on its own.

**Facts used:**
- Freehold, East Coast (Amber Gardens).
- 2-4 bedrooms, 538-1,744 sqft.
- From approx. $2,950 psf. Sources range $2,930-$3,030, so keep the "approx.".
- 5-min walk to Tanjong Katong MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line). Listing sites say 4-5 min, so the ads use the more conservative 5 min. Confirm with the client before launch.
