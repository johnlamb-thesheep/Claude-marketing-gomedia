> **Headline findings (read first)**
> 1. **Entry price is $1.546M (2BR, 678 sqft, $2,280 psf)**, per newlaunches.sg updated 06-Nov-2026 [NL]. That's **under the ~$1.6M line, so the Direct ad may lead with price.** Caveat: NL is the **only current source**. Stacked's launch figure was 2BR "from $1.499M" (Nov 2025) [SH-REV]. **Confirm against the developer's price list on the day the ad is built.**
> 2. **MRT is the weak point.** Hume MRT is ~0.87–0.90km straight-line [OM], and Stacked puts the walk at **~1.3km / ~19 min** [SH-REV]. **Don't run a walk-time MRT ad.** Reframe the MRT slot as nature/location.
> 3. **The hot USP is space + nature + resort facilities at a lower entry price than most city-fringe launches**: big layouts, Bukit Batok Nature Park across the road, and a 50m lap pool plus a full wellness deck [SH-REV].

## 6. Objections / risks

1. **No MRT within walking distance.** Stacked gives transport links 2.5/5 and calls it "would you trade MRT convenience for greenery?" [SH-REV], [GN-SEN].
2. **Limited daily amenities nearby**, so you need to drive [SH-REV].
3. **Single vehicle access point**, with traffic concerns as nearby plots develop; future density [SH-REV].
4. Layout nitpicks: tight 3BR master and wardrobe, small fridge space in the 3BR, standard 2.85m ceilings on most floors, no balcony water point [SH-REV].
5. **TOP date conflict** (2029 vs 2030). Sales should give the developer's date.
