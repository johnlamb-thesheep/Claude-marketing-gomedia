## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- Own-stay couples and families who drive, want more space for the money and like greenery more than a short MRT walk ([RES] §8 interpretation, Stacked's verdict). HDB and condo upgraders looking at the 3BR+Study and 4BR+Study.
- Budget from about S$1.5M (2-bedroom) up to S$3.5M+ (4-bedroom + study).

**The facts we highlight, and why**
1. **2-bedroom from $1.546M*** (USP 5). Entry is under the ~$1.6M line, so the Direct ad leads with price and the Price slot runs too. We print the exact figure, never a rounded one.
2. **Bigger homes: 2 to 4 bedrooms, 678 to 1,453 sqft** (USP 1). Stacked calls the sizes large for a new launch. The price is always paired with space or nature, so it isn't another bare "2-bedroom from $X" ad.
3. **Next to Bukit Batok Nature Park, in Upper Bukit Timah** (USP 2). The emotional hook and the replacement for the MRT ad.
4. **Layout details: windowed bathrooms in most homes, 3.9m ceilings on the 1st and 10th floors** (USP 4). The floor-plan ad.
5. **Resort wellness facilities: 50m lap pool, hydro pool, steam room, hot and cold baths, full-size tennis court** (USP 3). Supporting line in primary text.

**What competitors already say (so we stand apart)**
- The Ad Library barely surfaces this project. The only likely match is one Kier Gan page running 7 ads with "Get Free Price List & Ebrochure" headlines and no project name. Performance notes this is very likely our own agent-account campaign ("GoAhead The Sen adgen"), which the user needs to decide on before we launch.
- So: we lead with space, nature and an exact price, which no one is running here. Our CTA wording matches the norm, so the headline and visual have to do the differentiating.

**What we must NOT say**
- Walk minutes to any MRT. Stacked puts Hume MRT at ~1.3km / ~19 min, so we never say "near Hume MRT" as a walk. We say there's a bus stop outside with buses to Hume MRT and Beauty World MRT.
- A TOP date (sources conflict: 2029 vs 2030).
- "Award-winning" (award name unverified).
- 1-bedroom (sold out), and the old "from $993,900" / "from $1.18M" lines.
- "Within 1km" of any school. No P1 implication.
- Unit counts, % sold, "D21", street names (no "Jalan Jurong Kechil", no "Old Jurong Road"), land price or "lowest RCR land rate", sales-ranking lines ("#2 best-seller").
- Views for all units. Any discount (none found).
- Any price wording like "cheapest", "lowest", "deal" or "below market".

## 8. Flags (confirm before launch)

6. **"Award-winning"** (EdgeProp headline, 4 Nov 2026) isn't used until someone confirms which award.
7. **TOP** isn't printed (2029 vs 2030 conflict). Sales gives the developer's date.
8. **CEA authorisation.** If GoAhead isn't authorised to market The Sen, S1 is Blocked.
