### Ranking and wave plan (RECOMMENDATION ONLY — no spend without the user's approval in chat)

1. S1 Direct price-led. 2. S5 Price listing (blind). 3. S4 Nature + bus. 4. S2 Family. 5. S3 Layout.

- **Wave 1: S1, S5, S4.** S1 vs S5 is a clean named-vs-blind test at the same price; S4 tests price vs nature among the blind ads. **Wave 2:** S2, S3.
- **Structure:** 1 Leads campaign, Housing SAC, instant form, 1 ad set, broad / Advantage+, 4:5 + 9:16. **Only after G2** (don't stack on top of the live Kier Gan The Sen campaign without the user's decision).
- **Step 0:** re-contact the 46 past leads, and get the copy of the S$32.56 "The Sen" ad.
- **Budget:** ~S$50/day × 14 days (~S$700) → ~10–17 leads at S$40–75.
- **Qualified lead:** budget S$1.5M+, timeline ≤6 months, not an agent, answers WhatsApp within 72h; stage 2 = viewing booked.
- **Guardrails (heuristics; `ad-creative-fatigue` makes the final call):** no verdict before ~S$110 and 3 full days. Pause candidate: ~S$170 with 0 leads, or 0 of the first 6 leads at S$1.5M+. Scale candidate: CPL ≤ ~S$40 (near the old best ad) **and** ≥35% stage-1 qualified. Wave stop: S$700 with <3 stage-1 qualified leads. If the whole set runs above the old S$70 average, stop and rethink. Don't move on to wave 2.
- **Price trigger:** if the developer list shows no 2BR at ≤ ~$1.6M, S1 drops the price and S5 is pulled (copy Flag 2).
