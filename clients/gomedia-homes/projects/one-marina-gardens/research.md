## 6. Objections / risks to prepare for (sales + form, not ads)

1. **MRT not open, with no date** [SH-JUN], [SH-OCT]. This is the #1 objection, and competitor ads may be overpromising on it.
2. **Cheaper land next door:** the Marina Gardens Lane GLS site went to EL Development at **$1,243 psf ppr**, about 11–12% below OMG's ~$1,402 [SH-OCT], [GN-LANE]. Stacked says owners "may feel" they overpaid, and expects a future launch at ~$2,700–2,900 psf.
3. **Few amenities yet.** No schools within 1km, quiet evenings and weekends, limited parking (~433–445 lots), no tennis court, no private lifts [SH-JUN].
4. **Market sentiment:** Zaobao (4 Nov 2026) headline: "Marina Bay private home prices fall nearly 40%" [GN]. Not project-specific, but buyers will raise it.
5. Slow-sales narrative at launch (38%) [GN-OMG]. It's now ~74%, but we must not use % sold either way.

## 8. Observations vs interpretations

**Observations:**
- 8 competitor ads started 2–6 Nov 2026 [raw].
- Entry is $1.876M [NL], [SH-OCT].
- The Marina South MRT station isn't operating [SH-OCT].
- 245 units remain, mostly 2BR (135) [NL].
- It was #8 most-searched to 20 Oct with 5 deals in 14 days (per research-top-new-launches-2026-11.md).

**Interpretations:**
- Demand is mostly for 2BR stock, so the Direct and Blind ads should lean 2-bedroom while still showing the 646–1,647 sqft range. That's an inference.
- Competitors leaning on "MRT at your doorstep" leaves room for an accurate MRT ad that builds trust, but it's unproven.
- With price over the cap, the lead form must carry a budget band (S$1.8M+).
