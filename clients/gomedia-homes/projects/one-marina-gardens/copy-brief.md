## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- Own-stay couples and small families who want a new home in the city centre, close to Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay. Mostly 2-bedroom buyers (most of the remaining stock is 2BR, [RES] §8), with a smaller 3BR/4BR upgrader group.
- Budget S$1.9M and up. The entry is $1.876M, so **no price in any ad**. The lead form does the budget filtering.
- Not a schools buyer: there's no primary school within 1km and none planned ([RES] §3). No family/schools ad.

**The facts we highlight, and why**
1. **Next to Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay, with bay, garden and sea views from selected units** (USP 1). It's the main draw in every review, and no competitor uses it specifically.
2. **The first new homes in Marina South** (USP 2). A sourced, plain fact ([EP-25], [SH-OCT]). Used without "potential" or growth framing.
3. **Two working MRT stations nearby: Gardens by the Bay MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) and Marina South Pier MRT (North-South Line)** (USP 3). Honest MRT framing is our gap: a competitor is running "MRT at Your Doorstep", which leans on the station that isn't open.
4. **2 to 4 bedrooms, 646 to 1,647 sqft**, with curved master windows in the 3BR/4BR Premium and Smeg/Kohler fittings (USP 5). Floor-plan-led ads are open ground.
5. **3 pools including a 50m lap pool, and over 50 facilities across 6 levels** (USP 4). Replaces the Price slot.

**What competitors already say (so we stand apart)**
- 8 ads from 6 pages started 2–6 Nov: "Own a Home at One Marina Gardens", "Get the Live Price List", "Check Units Availability", "Discover Marina South's Potential!", "MRT at Your Doorstep", "Their Holiday Is Your Tuesday", "Explore Premium Homes in Marina South", and a comparison ad ("Two 3-bedrooms, S$411,300 apart").
- So: our Direct ad doesn't open on "Own a home at…" or "price list". Nobody names Gardens by the Bay MRT and Marina South Pier MRT, nobody shows a floor plan as the hero, and nobody leads on facilities. Comparison is taken here, so we don't use it.

**What we must NOT say**
- Any price, psf or monthly figure (entry $1.876M is over the ~$1.6M line). No "$" anywhere, fine print included.
- **Marina South MRT in any form.** It's built but not operating and has no opening date. Never "MRT at your doorstep", "MRT next door", "directly linked to MRT", and never draw it on a map. (Sales handles it as an objection; see Flag 3.)
- Walk minutes to any station (no source gives them). Write "near".
- 1-bedroom (sold out). Advertise 2 to 4 bedrooms only.
- Schools of any kind. "Investment potential", "growth", "undervalued", the Marina Gardens Lane land price, market-sentiment lines.
- Unit counts, % sold, "D01", street names (no "Marina Gardens Lane"). Showflat address (unverified).
- "Every unit has bay views". Views are always "from selected units".

## 2. Fixed on every ad

- Eyebrow `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER`.
- CTA `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE` (launched, price list released, [RES] §7). **Meta button: Download.** No preview, VVIP or launching-soon wording.
- Only O1 says "One Marina Gardens" or names the developer (primary text only). O2–O5 carry no name, developer or address, on the image or in the text.
- Base fine print on the image: `99-year leasehold. Distances approx., straight-line.` plus the image label.
- Image labels: `IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY` only on an official developer render once rights are cleared. Any stock or generated image gets `IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development` (or `– not the actual unit` for an interior). Maps: `Illustrative map, not to scale.` Never an AI tower captioned or implied as this project.
- A CEA particulars strip above the CTA on every ad. All text set in Canva/Figma, never by the image model. 4:5 and 9:16 versions.
- Word counts: headline ≤8, subhead ≤14 ("2–4", "646–1,647", "3-Bedroom", "50+", "Thomson-East", "North-South" and "&" count as one word each).

## 7. Plain-copy check

- Every headline says what it is (condo / layouts / MRT lines / facilities) and where (Marina South) or names the project.
- One hard fact on each: 2–4 bedrooms, 646–1,647 sqft, two named lines, 3 pools / 50+.
- No metaphors or invented terms. O2's question headline is a known gap against the plain-copy file, kept because the client asks for need-led question hooks.
- CTA = verb + what they get. Client wording kept exactly.

## 8. Flags (confirm before launch)

1. **Performance review done (2026-11-07, Revise).** OMG1 applied here; OMG2 is the designer's. Ready for re-review.
2. **Stock (within 48h of launch).** The size range 646–1,647 sqft assumes the 646 sqft 2BR and the 1,647 sqft 4BR Premium are still available ([NL] 07-Nov). If either sells out, update every range. The 3BR dual-key and the 4BR Premium are single-size types, so check them first.
3. **Marina South MRT.** Not in any ad. Sales needs a straight answer ready ("built, not yet open, no date announced by LTA"), because buyers will ask and competitors are implying it's open. Re-check its status on build day; if LTA announces an opening date, O1 and O4 are revisited first.
4. **"First new homes in Marina South" (O1 Headline B, O1 primary text).** Sourced to EdgeProp and Stacked. It stays true even when the Marina Gardens Lane site launches, but never write "only".
5. **CEA authorisation.** If GoAhead isn't authorised to market One Marina Gardens, O1 is Blocked.
6. **Rights.** Official renders and floor plans: O1 and O3 depend on them (O3's Headline B and sized tile titles need the official plans). Confirm GoAhead can send the developer's e-brochure, or the CTA promise breaks.
7. **Account history.** Performance found 154 past leads at S$54.32 (all paused), and a live "SG New Launch — Own a Home at One Marina Gardens" ad that may be our old copy on an agent account. The user should check that ad.
8. **Showflat address** is unverified, so no ad mentions the showflat location.
