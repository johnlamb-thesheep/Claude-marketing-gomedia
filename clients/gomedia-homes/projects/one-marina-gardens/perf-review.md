### Ranking and wave plan (RECOMMENDATION ONLY — no spend without the user's approval in chat)

1. O1 Direct — highest intent, and the account's best OMG ads were named. 2. O5 Facilities — best thumb-stop with an unused claim. 3. O2 Blind water — emotional volume; form must filter. 4. O4 Two MRT lines — the honest counter to competitors, weak hook. 5. O3 Size ladder — asset-gated.

- **Wave 1: O1, O5, O2** (named vs blind; concrete facilities vs emotional). **Wave 2:** O4, O3 (O3 once official plans clear). 
- **Structure:** 1 Leads campaign, Housing special ad category, instant form (website path failed on Arcady and Hougang), 1 ad set, broad / Advantage+ audience and placements, 4:5 + 9:16 each.
- **Step 0:** re-contact the 154 past OMG leads before any spend.
- **Budget:** ~S$50/day × 14 days (~S$700) → ~10–15 leads at S$45–70. It won't exit learning; it's a directional quality read.
- **Qualified lead (working definition):** budget S$1.8M+, timeline ≤6 months, not an agent, answers WhatsApp within 72h. Stage 2 = show-flat viewing booked.
- **Guardrails (heuristics; the final kill/scale/fatigue verdict comes from `ad-creative-fatigue`):** don't judge an ad before ~S$110 (≈2x expected CPL) and 3 full days (`conversion-lag-read-meta-ads`). Pause candidate: ~S$170 with 0 leads, or 0 of the first 5 leads at S$1.8M+. Scale candidate: CPL at or under the account's S$54 baseline **and** ≥35% stage-1 qualified. Wave stop: S$700 with <3 stage-1 qualified leads.
