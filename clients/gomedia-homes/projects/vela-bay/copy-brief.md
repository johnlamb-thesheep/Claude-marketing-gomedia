## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- **Primary:** own-stay couples and families who want the East Coast lifestyle (beach, park, food) and a train at the door. Most remaining stock is 3BR Premium and larger, so families with a ~$2.8M+ budget are the real target pool.
- **Secondary:** first-home singles/couples and small investors attracted by the 1-bedroom + study entry price (only 6 left, see Flag 1).
- Message direction only. No targeting by age, family status or income (Housing special ad category).

**Facts we highlight, and why**
1. **Next to Bayshore MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line).** #1 USP. The developer says "right at the doorstep", Stacked says "direct access", OneMap ~40m to Exit 1. Strong enough to say "next to" without minutes. [DEV], [SHR], [OM]
2. **East Coast Park a short walk away, through an underpass near the station.** #2 USP and the emotional hook. East Coast Lagoon Food Village nearby. [SHR], [DEV]
3. **1-bedroom + study from $1.47M\*** (cheapest unit $1,466,000, 484 sqft). Under the ~$1.5M line, so the Direct ad leads with price. [NL] 06-Nov-2026
4. **3-bedroom Premium, 1,033 sqft: enclosed wet kitchen, separate yard, household shelter, extra WC.** This is where most stock sits, so the floor-plan ad points at units that exist. [SHA], [SHR], [DEV]
5. **Sea views from selected units** (no percentage), homes 484–1,765 sqft, expected TOP 2031. [SHR], [NL]

**What competitors already say**
- The Ad Library found **no Vela Bay-named ads**. A `Bayshore condo` re-pull found one unnamed price teaser ("Prices Starts From $1.4XXM", SG New Launches) and one "Bayshore's Town Centre" ad (Nouvel Homes).
- So: price is the only angle seen. **Nobody is running MRT + East Coast Park, a floor-plan hero, or a beach/family hook.** We lead with those and keep price to the Direct and Price slots.

**What we must NOT say**
- Walk minutes to Bayshore MRT (no source gives minutes). Stacked's "2–3 minutes" is from the underpass, not the condo, so it isn't printed either.
- Any sea-view share ("over 70%" vs "about 50%" conflict). Only "sea views from selected units".
- The developer's "7 mins walk" figure (unclear what it refers to).
- "10 MRT stops to the CBD" (developer-only claim). "First condo in the new Bayshore precinct", "record", "best".
- Any discount (none found). Growth, profit or "D16 deals profitable" framing.
- % sold, unit counts, "only X left", block counts, "D16", street address (Bayshore Walk).
- Preview/VVIP wording (launched Apr 2026).
- The 2BR ($1.695M) and larger types' prices. They're above the cap, so only the 1-bedroom + study entry figure appears.

- Eyebrow `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER`.
- CTA `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE`. Meta button **Download**.

## 6. Flags — confirm before launch
1. **The price only works while the 1-bedroom + study lasts.** 6 of those units were left on 06-Nov ([NL]). If they're gone, the next entry is the 2BR at $1.695M, which is above the cap, so **no price anywhere**: V1 switches to Headline B (no price block), and V5 is retired. Check within 48h of launch.
2. **Price-led ads steer buyers to a nearly sold-out type.** Most stock is 3BR Premium and larger. The form's budget/unit-type questions must catch this, and sales should be ready to offer the 3BR Premium. Performance has put V5 on hold (wave 1: V1, V3, V2).
3. **Price source is an agent portal ([NL]), not the developer.** QA confirms on the developer's current list that a 1-bedroom + study is available at ≤$1,470,000 and fills [DATE].
4. **CEA authorisation** for GoAhead to market Vela Bay. If not authorised, V1 is Blocked.
5. **Rights** to the official render and the 3BR Premium plan (V1, V3). V3 Headline A needs the official plan; without it, use Headline B and the illustrative title (VB3). A floor-plan ad paired with a non-building photo needs the user's OK in chat before launch (perf ruling 3).
6. **velabay.com.sg operator unconfirmed** (no CEA number). Don't link it or use its assets until cleared.
7. **Account history:** performance found past "GoAhead Vela Bay / Chuan Grove" activity on an agent account, but no figures. Run the read-only pull and check for old leads to re-contact before launch.
8. ~~"A short walk" / "rooftop clubhouse" wording~~ **Closed:** performance allowed both (ruling 1).
