## How to use these prompts

**Recommended route (perf S2, used on every earlier project): photo plate + live text.**
1. Generate only the photo (the "Photo plate" line under each ad), with no text in it.
2. Build the layout, panels, chips and buttons in Canva or Figma using the exact hex values.
3. Type every string yourself and proof each character against `copy-brief.md`. Image models garble small text and prices.
4. Final typeset: fine print at **18 px or more** at 1080 wide (perf note, Coastal Cabana 2026-11-07).

**Quick mock-up route:** paste the full prompt (4:5) into ChatGPT (image) or Gemini. It draws the whole ad with text. Use that only to see the layout. Never publish its rendered text without retyping and proofing it. Then paste the 9:16 line into the same chat.

## Rules baked into every prompt

- **Only V1 shows "Vela Bay".** V2–V5 show no project name, developer, developer entity, street, "Bayshore Walk" or logo, in text or image.
- No price except `$1.47M*` on V1 and V5 (entry ~$1.466M, under the ~$1.5M line). No psf, discount, monthly figure, other unit-type prices.
- No unit counts, block counts, storey counts, "% sold", "only X left", district codes ("D16"), street names, "first condo in the precinct", "record".
- No walk minutes or metres anywhere. Bayshore MRT is "next to" / "next door"; East Coast Park is "a short walk".
- Full station names: "Bayshore MRT", "Marine Parade MRT". Line: "Thomson-East Coast Line". No real line colours (the TEL brown included), no LTA/MRT logos, no station codes (TE29 etc.).
- No sea-view percentage. Sea views are always "from selected units".
- **No AI-generated condo tower, facade or skyline on any ad.** Sea is **soft grey-green**, never turquoise (perf CC6, same coast as Coastal Cabana). No jetties, landmarks, the East Coast Lagoon Food Village building or signage, or offshore islands. No identifiable faces.
- Image labels (perf T10): generic or generated photos carry `IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development` (or `– not the actual unit` for an interior). `IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY` only on an official developer render. Diagrams: `Illustrative diagram, not to scale.` Illustrative plan: `ILLUSTRATIVE LAYOUT ONLY, not to scale`.
- Floor plan: a generic line drawing with **no text, numbers or dimensions inside it**, until the client supplies the official 3BR Premium plan.
- A clear ~60 px strip above every CTA is reserved for the CEA particulars.
- Brand constants: red `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER` eyebrow (#D0021B), CTA `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE` (Meta button **Download**). Everything else changes per ad.
- 9:16 (1080x1920): no text in the top 14% (~270 px) or the bottom 20% (~385 px).
