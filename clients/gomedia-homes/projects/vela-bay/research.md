## 4. Ad Library analysis

- **Pull result:** no relevant Vela Bay ads surfaced for either search form [raw pull]. No pages, headlines or CTAs to analyse.
- **Interpretation:** this could be a search gap (the API matches odd terms), or a genuinely quiet auction now that the launch push is six months old. **The lead should re-run `Bayshore condo` and `Bayshore MRT` before calling the auction "empty".** Per learnings.md, the Ad Library also misses our own paused ads, so performance should check the account for past GoAhead Vela Bay campaigns.
- **Read-across from the custom-list research** (SKILL.md): bedroom + price headlines are saturated market-wide. For Vela Bay specifically, no price, seafront or MRT angle could be confirmed as crowded.
- **Likely gaps GoAhead can own:** "next to Bayshore MRT + East Coast Park" as a pair; a floor-plan hero of the 3BR Premium (enclosed kitchen + yard), where most stock sits; and a family "beach after school" hook.

## 5. Objections / risks to prepare for

1. **The neighbourhood is still being built.** Stacked: "buyers have to go on trust"; there are few shops or cafés today [SHR].
2. **Long trips to the city centre.** Stacked: Orchard/Raffles Place are "still a relatively long trip"; the airport needs two train changes [SHR].
3. **Future supply nearby:** the Bayshore Drive mixed-use GLS site (~1,280 homes, $2.13B Frasers-led bid, Jul 2026) [GN-VB], [SHB]. **Bayshore BTO flats are in the Nov 2026 BTO exercise** [GN-B].
4. **Record OCR-east psf** (~$2,700–2,900 psf); PropertyNet asked whether Vela Bay sets an "OCR price record" [GN-VB]. Older neighbours (Bayshore Park, The Bayshore) are much cheaper per psf [SHPR].
5. **Small second bedrooms** (~98 sqft) in 2BR/3BR units; standard 2BR/3BR have no yard or shelter [SHR].
6. **Stock is skewed large:** the entry types are nearly gone (6 × 1BR+S, 4 × 2BR) [NL].
7. ECP noise: Stacked says it is "unlikely to be noticeable" [SHR]. A minor point.

## 6. Recommendations for the copywriter (data, not strategy)

**CTA:** "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE". The price list is released and the project launched in Apr 2026. **No preview/VVIP wording.**

**Top 5 facts for the 5 angles**
1. **Direct:** "Vela Bay" + "1-bedroom + study from approx. $1.47M*" + "next to Bayshore MRT". Fine print: "*Price as at 6 Nov 2026, subject to availability and change." Under the price rule this is allowed. Remaining stock is thin, so the form should ask about unit type and budget.
2. **Blind (need-led):** "Looking for a home by the sea?" → "New condo in Bayshore, next to Bayshore MRT, East Coast Park via underpass." No name.
3. **Floor layout:** 3BR Premium, 1,033 sqft: enclosed wet kitchen, yard, household shelter, extra WC. This is where the stock is. A real floor plan is needed (rights to confirm).
4. **MRT:** "Next to Bayshore MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line)", paired with "East Coast Park & East Coast Lagoon Food Village nearby". **No minutes.**
5. **Price:** "1-bedroom + study homes in Bayshore from approx. $1.47M*" (no name). Alternatively, the size range "484–1,765 sqft homes by East Coast Park".

**Safe supporting lines:** "Sea views from selected units". "Size range 484–1,765 sqft". "Expected TOP 2031". "Near Temasek Primary School" plus the MOE line.

**UNVERIFIED / do not use**
- Walk minutes to Bayshore MRT (no source gives minutes).
- "Over 70% sea-facing" (developer) vs "about 50%" (Stacked): don't quantify sea views.
- The developer's "7 mins walk" figure (layout unclear; can't tell what it refers to).
- Any discount (none found).
- "First / record" price claims, "best new launch".
- "10 MRT stops to CBD" is a developer claim. Use it only if attributed and checked by QA.
- **Never:** % sold, unit counts, "D16", "only X units left", growth claims (e.g. EdgeProp's "96% of D16 deals profitable" headline).

## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations:** the Ad Library pull returned no Vela Bay ads. The project is ~77% sold, with stock concentrated in 3BR Premium and larger (95 of 120 units left are 3BR Premium or bigger). The entry price is $1.466M (1BR+S, 6 left). OneMap puts the towers ~40m from Bayshore MRT Exit 1. EdgeProp ran a developer-style feature on 4 Nov 2026 ("seafront homes… yacht-inspired design"), which suggests a renewed marketing push.

**Interpretations (my read; strategist to decide):** a price-led Direct is allowed but steers leads to sold-down types. The MRT + East Coast Park pair is the strongest and most factual hook. The quiet Ad Library may mean an open auction, but this is unproven until a wider keyword pull is done.
