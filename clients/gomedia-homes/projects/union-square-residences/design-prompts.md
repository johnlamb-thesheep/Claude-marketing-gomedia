## Rules baked into every prompt

- **Only U1 shows "Union Square Residences".** U2–U5 show no name, developer (CDL), street (Havelock Road), the word "Union" or any logo.
- Price only `$1.31M*` (1-bedroom, entry $1,307,000) on U1 and U5, and `$1.42M*` (1-bedroom + study) on U3. No psf, discount, "savings", "price cut", 2BR or 3BR prices.
- No unit counts ("366"), shop counts, "% sold", "76 left", "23 sold", "District 1", "D01", "CCR", "New PR", residency/CPF hooks, rental yield or investor language, Clarke Quay rejuvenation as fact, preview/VVIP wording, river views "from every unit".
- No walk minutes to any MRT or mall. Full station names: Clarke Quay MRT, Chinatown MRT, Fort Canning MRT, Dhoby Ghaut MRT. Lines in full: North East Line, Downtown Line. **No real line colours** (NEL purple, DTL blue and the rest), no LTA/MRT logos, no station codes.
- **No AI-generated condo tower, facade or identifiable skyline.** No mosque, temple, shophouse frontage or named neighbouring building in any frame. No identifiable faces. No real shop logos.
- Image labels (perf T10): generic photos `IMAGE SAMPLE ONLY – not the actual development`. `IMAGE SAMPLE / ARTIST'S IMPRESSION ONLY` only on an official render. Map/diagram: `Illustrative map, not to scale.` Plan: `ILLUSTRATIVE LAYOUT ONLY, not to scale`.
- Floor plan: generic line drawing with **no text, numbers or dimensions inside it** until the official 1BR + Study plan is supplied.
- ~60 px clear strip above every CTA for the CEA particulars. Brand constants: red `SINGAPORE HOMEBUYER` eyebrow (#D0021B) and `GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE` (Meta button **Download**).
- 9:16: no text in the top 14% or the bottom 20%.
