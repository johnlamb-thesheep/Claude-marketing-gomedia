## 5. Objections / risks to prepare for

1. **Clarke Quay's decline:** F&B operators have exited, citing "rising rents, stiff alcohol duties, and high labour costs" [SHC]. Rejuvenation is still a plan.
2. **Views:** lower-floor river views are blocked by Raffles Education Square and Riverside Piazza. Possible redevelopment of neighbours could change the views [SHR].
3. **Neighbours:** a mosque opposite and a temple near the co-living block [SHR].
4. **Small site:** no tennis court or jogging track; parking for ~51% of units; standard 2.75m ceilings; tight 2BR study (~3 sqm) and no-window common bathrooms [SHR].
5. **Price vs resale:** ~30% above resale peers (Feb 2025); only 3 of the 1BRs sold by Feb 2025, "a poor sign of either the layout, its price or both" [SHP]. The 1BR entry has since dropped.
6. **Investor-heavy mix** (74% are 1–2BR) [SHR]. Rental/yield questions will come up, but **never answer them in ads** (no returns claims).
7. **Sales-status conflict:** 48% vs 79%. Buyers may quote either. Don't engage in ads.

## 6. Recommendations for the copywriter (data, not strategy)

**CTA:** "GET PRICE LIST & E-BROCHURE". Launched Nov 2024. No preview/VVIP wording.

**Top 5 facts for the 5 angles**
1. **Direct:** "Union Square Residences" + "1-bedroom from approx. $1.31M*" + "near Clarke Quay MRT & Chinatown MRT, by CDL". Fine print: "*Price as at 7 Nov 2026, subject to availability and change."
2. **Blind (need-led):** "Your first home in the city?" → "New riverside condo in Clarke Quay with a sky pool & mall downstairs, near 3 MRT stations." No name.
3. **Floor layout:** 1BR + Study, 506 sqft (most entry stock: 30 left), or the 2BR + Study B5S, 743 sqft. Real plan needed (rights TBC).
4. **MRT:** "Near Clarke Quay MRT, Chinatown MRT & Fort Canning MRT", plus "Chinatown Point & The Central nearby". **For minutes**, the only source is Stacked's "7–8 min walk" (2024, not the developer). Either omit minutes, or write "approx. 8-min walk to Clarke Quay MRT" with fine print "Walk time approx., per third-party review". **QA to decide.**
5. **Price:** "1-bedroom homes by the Singapore River from approx. $1.31M*" (no name).

**Safe supporting lines:** "Expected completion 2029". "Sky pool on level 34". "Mixed-use: shops & offices within the development". "Homes from 463 sqft". "Near River Valley Primary School" plus the MOE line.

**UNVERIFIED / do not use**
- "6-figure savings", any discount (not confirmed by CDL).
- 3BR availability.
- % sold, "76 left", "23 sold in 7 days" (rule; disputed).
- Rental yield, "investor favourite", "Prime HDB" growth drivers, "price will rise".
- River views from every unit (only selected/higher floors).
- Walk minutes to malls (NL vs Stacked conflict).
- Clarke Quay rejuvenation as fact. Only "planned" with hedging, if at all.
- **Never:** "District 1"/"D01", unit counts ("366 units"), "CCR".

## Observations vs interpretations

**Observations:** 4 relevant advertisers. Huttons' agent page started 3 ads on 6 Nov (price, District 1, scarcity). The entry price ($1.307M) is lower than at launch. Sales figures conflict (48% vs 79%). OneMap puts Clarke Quay MRT and Chinatown MRT exits ~270–280m away in straight line.

**Interpretations (my read; strategist to decide):** the price-led Direct is allowed but will compete head-on with Huttons' "From S$1.31M". The MRT-trio and river/sky-pool angles are uncontested. The falling entry price suggests the developer is pushing balance units, but that's an inference: confirm any promotion with CDL or the appointed marketing agency before using it.
