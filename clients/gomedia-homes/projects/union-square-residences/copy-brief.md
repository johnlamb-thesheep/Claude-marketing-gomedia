## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- **Primary:** first-home singles and couples who work in or near the city and want a train and a mall on their doorstep. The entry types are 1-bedroom (from $1.307M) and 1-bedroom + study (from $1.417M, 30 left: the deepest entry stock).
- **Secondary:** small investors (74% of the mix is 1–2BR) and right-sizers. Investor interest is expected, but **no yield, rental or returns language** anywhere.
- Message direction only. No targeting by age, residency, nationality or income (Housing special ad category). We don't copy the competitor's "New PR?" hook.

**Facts we highlight, and why**
1. **Three MRT stations on two lines nearby:** Clarke Quay MRT and Chinatown MRT (North East Line), Chinatown MRT and Fort Canning MRT (Downtown Line). #1 USP, and **no competitor uses it**. Said as "near", no minutes. [OM], [NL], [SHR]
2. **1-bedroom from $1.31M\*** (cheapest unit $1,307,000, 463 sqft). Well under the cap, so Direct and Price lead with it. [NL] 07-Nov-2026, [NLH]
3. **Riverside living with a level-34 sky pool** looking over the Singapore River, plus a 30m lap pool, gym and co-working studio. Only the developer's 2025 piazza ad touches lifestyle. [SHR], [SHL]
4. **Mixed-use: shops on the lower floors and an office tower in the development**, with Chinatown Point, The Central and People's Park Centre nearby. [SHR], [NL]
5. **1-bedroom + study, 506 sqft, from $1.42M\*** as the floor-plan hero, plus developer CDL (Direct ad, primary text only). [SHL], [NL], [GN-US2 via RES]

**What competitors already say** ([AL])
- **SG Condo Insider By Huttons** (3 ads, started 6 Nov): "Union Square Floor Plans: 76 of 366 Left", "23 Units Sold in 7 Days at Union Square", "New PR? District 1 From S$1.31M With Your CPF".
- **Singapore Properties:** "Limited Units with 6-Figure Savings" (May 2026).
- **Developer-style page:** "A public piazza / Artist Impression of the night / Union Club" (Nov 2025).
- **Joel Kwok:** "Download 2026's Underrated Condo List".
- **So:** our "from $1.31M\*" Direct and Price ads sit right next to Huttons' price ad. We separate them with a second fact Huttons doesn't use (3 MRT stations, the river and sky pool) and with no scarcity, PR or district wording. "Floor plans" is used only as a units-left headline, so a real floor-plan hero is still open.

**What we must NOT say**
- "6-figure savings", any discount or "price cut" (the entry is lower than at launch, but CDL hasn't confirmed a discount).
- 3-bedroom anything (availability unknown).
- % sold, "76 left", "23 sold in 7 days", unit counts ("366 units"), the shop count.
- "District 1", "D01", "CCR", the street address (Havelock Road).
- Walk minutes to any MRT or mall (perf ruling 5). "A short walk" for malls: use "nearby" (perf US2).
- River views from every unit (only the sky pool, and "selected units").
- Clarke Quay rejuvenation as fact. Rental yield, "investor favourite", "price will rise".
- Preview/VVIP wording (launched Nov 2024).
- The 2BR ($2.0M) and larger types' prices.
- "MRT" without a named station in headlines, tiles or badges (perf US3/US4).
