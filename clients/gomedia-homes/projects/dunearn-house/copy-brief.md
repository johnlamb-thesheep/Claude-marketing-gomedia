## 1. Copy brief

**Who the buyer is**
- Two groups, because only 2-bedroom and 4-bedroom homes are left (3BR sold out):
  - **2BR buyers:** couples and singles who want a new Bukit Timah address near the Downtown Line. Budget from about S$1.5M up to ~S$2M.
  - **4BR buyers:** HDB and condo upgrader families (ERA noted Queenstown upgraders) who want space near the Bukit Timah schools. Budget S$4M+. This is the bigger stock pool.

**The facts we highlight, and why**
1. **The first new condo in Bukit Timah's Turf City precinct, by Frasers Property, CSC Land Group and Sekisui House** (USP 1 + 3). Direct ad only. Developer names stay in primary text, so the ad never reads as the developer's own.
2. **Near Sixth Avenue MRT on the Downtown Line**, with Turf City MRT on the Cross Island Line planned for 2032 (USP 2). The strongest MRT fact of the four projects (~0.45–0.5km straight-line), but still no walk minutes.
3. **The Bukit Timah school belt** (USP 4). The emotional family hook for the 4BR stock. Nobody advertising this project uses schools.
4. **Green resort living: two clubhouses, a 50m lap pool, the Mirage Forest and canopy trail, about a third of the site as greenery, near the Rail Corridor and Bukit Timah Nature Reserve** (USP 5). Replaces the Price slot. Nobody uses it.
5. **Practical layouts: 2-bedroom from 527 sqft, 4-bedroom up to 1,378 sqft, most homes with kitchen or bathroom windows, 2.875m ceilings** (USP 6). The floor-plan ad.

**Price decision: no price in any running ad** (Approved by performance). The research marks the $1.538M entry as **conditional and conflicting**: newlaunches.sg says $1.538M (06-Nov), Stacked's 28 Aug table says 2BR from $1.91M, only 9 of that 527 sqft type are left, and the next 2BR is $1.848M (over the line). A **held** price-led swap for D1 is in section 5 and goes in only after the developer's price list confirms it. The Price slot becomes green resort living.

**What competitors already say (so we stand apart)**
- 10 ads from 7 pages: "Check 2BR / 4BR Available Units" (the project's own page), "First Mover in Turf City" (the developer), "Missed Dunearn House's 3-bedders?" and "S$435K less for a new-launch 3-bedder" (a competitor conquesting the 3BR sell-out), "2BR new-launch home in Bukit Timah Under $2M", stale preview and pre-launch ads, and a price-list ad.
- So: no availability, no "first mover", no "under $2M", no 3BR anything. We take green living, schools, a floor-plan hero and the honest MRT map, none of which are in use.

**What we must NOT say**
- **Any 3-bedroom offer.** 2BR and 4BR only. Never conquest the 3BR sell-out.
- The $1.538M price (held until confirmed), any "under $2M" line, psf, monthly figures, discounts.
- MRT walk minutes (none published; the route crosses Dunearn Road and the canal). Turf City MRT as open; it's always "planned" with the 2032 target.
- "Within 1km" of any school, any school admission implication, a single school in a headline, and schools with no sourced distance (Nanyang Primary, Henry Park Primary) by name.
- "First in 33 years", "first-mover advantage", growth or potential talk. Bukit V mall as open. The Green Corridor as complete, or on any image.
- Unit counts, % sold, "D11", street names (no "Dunearn Road"). "Dunearn" appears only inside the project name, on D1.
- Preview or launching-soon wording (launched July 2026).
