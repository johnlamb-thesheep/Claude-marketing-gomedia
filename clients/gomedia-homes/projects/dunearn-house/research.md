## 6. Objections / risks

1. **Price above nearby resale, including some freehold** [SH-REV1]. The cheapest 2BR is compact (527 sqft).
2. **Years of construction:** the Turf City estate isn't complete until the 2040s; noise and dust for stacks facing Turf City and Dunearn Road; future supply of ~20,000 homes [SH-REV1], [SH-REV2].
3. **No walkable mall** (the old Turf City/Grandstand closed in 2023; Bukit V is expected ~2028) [SH-REV1], [SH-REV2].
4. **Parking at ~60% of units** (228 lots) [NL], [SH-REV2].
5. Lease decay during construction; non-PPVC study is small [SH-REV1], [SH-REV2].
6. **Wanted a 3BR?** They're sold out. Sales should have 2BR+Study / 4BR alternatives ready. Competitors are already targeting these buyers.
